1Fault strength (effective stress)斷層強度（有效應力）
\[ \tau_f = c + \mu(\sigma_n - u) \]

μ: friction coefficient; σn: normal stress on the fault; u: pore pressure.Raising u lowers the strength and can trigger slip.

μ 摩擦係數、σn 斷層面正應力、u 孔隙水壓；孔壓上升會降低強度、誘發錯動。

2Seismic moment M0地震矩
\[ M_0 = \mu AD \]

μ: rigidity of the crust (≈ 3×10¹⁰ N/m² = 3×10¹¹ dyne/cm²); A: rupture area; D: average slip.Units: N·m or dyne·cm (1 N·m = 10⁷ dyne·cm).

μ 地殼剛性模數、A 破裂面積、D 平均滑移量；單位 N·m 或 dyne·cm。

2.6 →練習 1.1
3Moment magnitude Mw地震矩規模
\[ \begin{aligned} &M_w = \tfrac23(\log M_0 - 9.05)\quad (M_0\ \text{in N}\cdot\text{m}) \\ &M_w = \frac{\log M_0 - 16.05}{1.5}\quad (M_0\ \text{in dyne}\cdot\text{cm}) \end{aligned} \]

Use the constant that matches the unit of M0.Mw does not saturate.

常數要配合 M0 的單位；Mw 不會飽和。

規模差 1 → 地震矩 31.6 倍；差 2 → 1000 倍。

#名稱公式與說明推導／練習
5Integration in the frequency domain★積分 = 除以 iω
\[ V = \frac{A}{i\omega},\qquad U = -\frac{A}{\omega^2},\qquad PGV \approx \frac{PGA}{\omega} = \frac{PGA}{2\pi f} \]

Integration amplifies low frequencies: a constant bias ε grows to u ≈ ½εt².PGV ≈ PGA/ω holds for a harmonic motion of frequency f.

積分放大低頻，常數偏差會變成漂移；PGV ≈ PGA/ω 只適用簡諧運動。

6Nyquist frequency and resolution★Nyquist 頻率與頻率解析度
\[ f_N = \frac{1}{2\Delta t},\qquad \Delta f = \frac{1}{N\Delta t} \]

Δt: sampling interval; N: number of points.Content above fN is aliased.

Δt 取樣間隔、N 點數；高於 fN 的成分會疊頻。

7Arias intensity Ia★Arias 強度
\[ \begin{aligned} &I_a = \frac{\pi}{2g}\int_0^{t_d} a^2\,dt \\ &\int_{T}(A\sin\omega t)^2dt = \frac{A^2T}{2}\ (\text{integer cycles}) \end{aligned} \]

Unit m/s; scales with amplitude squared (a × k → Ia × k²).

單位 m/s；振幅放大 k 倍，Ia 變 k² 倍。正弦整數週期的積分是 A²T/2。

8Cumulative absolute velocity CAV★累積絕對速度
\[ CAV = \int_0^{t_d}|a|\,dt,\qquad \int_{T}|A\sin\omega t|\,dt = \frac{2}{\pi}AT \]

Scales linearly with amplitude.CAV5 accumulates only the parts with |a| ≥ 5 cm/s².

與振幅成正比；CAV5 只累積 |a| ≥ 5 cm/s² 的部分。

9Durations Db, Du, D5–95★三種延時
\[ \begin{aligned} &D_b = t_{last} - t_{first} \\ &D_u = \sum \Delta t_i\,(|a| \gt a_t) \\ &D_{5\text{-}95} = t_{95\%} - t_{5\%} \end{aligned} \]

Db: first to last exceedance of the threshold at (0.05 g or 5 % PGA).Du: sum of the intervals above at; Du ≤ Db.D5–95: from the Husid plot (5 % to 95 % of Ia).

Db 第一次到最後一次超過門檻；Du 超過門檻的時段加總；D5–95 由 Husid 圖讀 5 % 到 95 %。

#名稱公式與說明推導／練習
12Elastic constants彈性常數換算
\[ \begin{aligned} &G = \frac{E}{2(1+\nu)} \\ &M = \frac{E(1-\nu)}{(1+\nu)(1-2\nu)} \\ &\nu = \frac{E}{2G} - 1 \end{aligned} \]

M: constrained modulus (zero lateral strain).

M 為束制模數（側向應變為零）。

6.2 →練習 3.1練習 N.1練習 N.17
13Wave velocities波速
\[ V_p = \sqrt{\frac{M}{\rho}},\qquad V_s = \sqrt{\frac{G}{\rho}},\qquad v_c = \sqrt{\frac{E}{\rho}}\ (\text{rod}) \]

Vp: P wave in an infinite medium; vc: longitudinal wave in a laterally unconfined rod.Vs < vc < Vp.

無限介質的 P 波用 M；側向自由的細桿縱波用 E。

14Vp/Vs ratio波速比
\[ \frac{V_p}{V_s} = \sqrt{\frac{2-2\nu}{1-2\nu}} \]

Depends only on ν; in saturated soil Vp is controlled by the pore water, not the soil skeleton.

只與 ν 有關；飽和土 ν 接近 0.5，Vp 由孔隙水控制。

17Strain, particle velocity and impedance應變、質點速度與阻抗
\[ \sigma = \rho V\dot u,\qquad \varepsilon = \frac{\dot u}{V_p},\qquad \gamma = \frac{\dot u}{V_s} \approx \frac{PGV}{V_s},\qquad Z = \rho V \]

Valid for a traveling plane wave.Z = ρV: specific impedance (stress / particle velocity).

18Reflection and transmission (normal incidence)介面的反射與透射
\[ \begin{aligned} &\alpha = \frac{Z_2}{Z_1} \\ &\frac{A_t}{A_i} = \frac{2}{1+\alpha},\qquad \frac{A_r}{A_i} = \frac{1-\alpha}{1+\alpha} \\ &\frac{\sigma_t}{\sigma_i} = \frac{2\alpha}{1+\alpha},\qquad \frac{\sigma_r}{\sigma_i} = -\frac{1-\alpha}{1+\alpha} \end{aligned} \]

Medium 1 is the incident side.Hard → soft (α < 1): displacement amplified, stress reduced.Free surface (α = 0): At = 2Ai.
