名詞解釋

作答順序：一句定義 → 公式 → 一兩個關鍵性質。2025 年的格式是「briefly describe in English/Chinese what you know about…」，每題 4 分；Modulus reduction curve 最好順手畫一條 S 形曲線、標出 γtl 與 γr。

A　看一個迴圈

先畫一個遲滯迴圈：勁度看斜率、阻尼看面積。圖中 ①–⑤ 對應下表的名詞。

#名詞說明詳細推導
①Hysteresis loop遲滯迴圈

The closed τ–γ curve traced by one cycle of cyclic loading with strain amplitude γc.Its inclination (origin to tip) gives the stiffness Gsec; its area is the energy dissipated per cycle ED, which gives the damping ratio.As γc increases, the loop both lies down (lower Gsec) and becomes fatter (higher D).Shape is also information: a slender ellipse ≈ linear viscoelastic; a banana-shaped loop with pointed ends indicates high pore pressure and dilation.

一個反覆載重週期在 τ–γ 圖上畫出的閉合曲線。斜度給勁度、面積給阻尼；應變越大，迴圈越躺、越胖。細長橢圓接近線性黏彈性；兩端尖、中間細腰的香蕉形代表孔隙水壓已高、土壤在剪脹。

8.3 →
②Backbone curve骨幹曲線

The stress–strain curve for initial (first) loading, τ = f(γ).Its initial slope is Gmax and it approaches the shear strength τmax at large strain.The tips of loops of all amplitudes lie on it, so Gsec = f(γ)/γ: the backbone curve and the modulus reduction curve carry the same information.

初次載重的 τ–γ 曲線，起始斜率 Gmax、上限 τmax。各幅度迴圈的尖端都落在骨幹上，所以骨幹與模數折減曲線是同一件事的兩種表示。

8.3 →8.6 →
③Secant and tangent shear modulus割線與切線剪力模數

Secant modulus \(G_{sec} = \tau_c/\gamma_c\): the slope from the origin to the loop tip; the average stiffness of the whole cycle, used in equivalent linear analysis.Tangent modulus \(G_{tan} = d\tau/d\gamma\) at a point on the loop: highest (≈ Gmax) just after a reversal and nearly flat near the tip; used step by step in nonlinear time-domain analysis.Gsec decreases with increasing strain amplitude.

割線模數是原點到迴圈尖端的斜率，代表整圈的平均勁度，等值線性法用它；切線模數是迴圈上某點的斜率，剛反轉時最陡（≈ Gmax）、接近尖端變平，非線性分析每一步用它。

8.3 →
④Small-strain shear modulus Gmax小應變剪力模數

The shear modulus at strains below the linear threshold, where G no longer depends on strain.\(G_{max} = \rho V_s^2\), obtained from field or laboratory Vs (Ch7).It is the anchor of the modulus reduction curve: G = (G/Gmax)·Gmax. It depends mainly on mean effective stress (∝ σ′m0.5), void ratio, OCR, age and cementation.

低於線性門檻應變時的剪力模數，\(G_{max} = \rho V_s^2\)，由波速量得（單元 7）。它是模數折減曲線的錨點；主要受平均有效應力、孔隙比、OCR、年代與膠結影響。

7.1 →8.5 →
⑤Equivalent damping ratio DHW3等值（遲滯）阻尼比

The damping ratio of a viscous system that dissipates the same energy per cycle at resonance as the soil loop.\(D = E_D/(4\pi E_{So})\): ED = loop area, \(E_{So} = \tfrac12\tau_c\gamma_c\) = the triangle from the origin to the tip.Soil damping is hysteretic (friction between particles), nearly independent of frequency, so the frequency ratio of the viscous result is taken as 1.Dividing by ESo removes the load level, as cc does for c in SDOF; D is the same quantity as ξ.

借用黏滯阻尼器：找一個共振時每圈耗能與土壤迴圈相同的黏滯系統，以它的阻尼比當土壤的等值阻尼比。土壤是與頻率幾乎無關的遲滯阻尼，所以直接取 ω/ωn = 1。分母的三角形把載重大小去掉，角色等同 SDOF 的 cc。

B　看兩條曲線

把不同應變振幅的迴圈整理成兩條曲線，橫軸一律是對數剪應變。圖中 ⑥–⑬ 對應下表的名詞。

#名詞說明詳細推導
⑥Shear strain ranges剪應變範圍（Ishihara）

Small strain (< 10−3 %): nearly linear elastic, G ≈ Gmax; linear analysis (machine foundations, traffic vibration).Medium strain (10−3–10−1 %): nonlinear and hysteretic but not failing, properties do not change with cycles; viscoelastic model, equivalent linear method.Large strain (> 10−1 %): failure possible, number-of-cycles and rate effects are significant; nonlinear step-by-step time-domain analysis.Ishihara's original limits are in decimal (10−5, 10−3), i.e. 100 times smaller than the % values.

小應變近乎線彈性、用線性分析；中應變非線性、有遲滯但不破壞，用等值線性法；大應變可能破壞、圈數與速率效應明顯，要用時間域逐步積分。原表的 10−5 是小數，等於 10−3 %。

8.2 →
⑦Threshold shear strains γtl and γtvHW3線性與體積門檻剪應變

Linear threshold γtl ≈ 10−3 %: below it the soil is nearly linear elastic, G ≈ Gmax and D ≈ Dmin.Volumetric threshold γtv ≈ 10−2 % for sands (up to about 0.1 % for high-PI clays): above it particles slide and rearrange, causing permanent volume change (drained) or excess pore pressure (undrained), and properties change with cycles.Both increase with PI, so plastic clays are less prone to pore-pressure build-up; liquefaction and seismic settlement start only above γtv.

低於 γtl 近乎線彈性；介於兩者之間非線性但無永久體積變化；高於 γtv 時排水產生體積變化、不排水累積超額孔隙水壓。兩者都隨 PI 增加，所以黏土較不易累積孔隙水壓、較不易液化。

8.5 →
⑧Modulus reduction curve★HW3模數折減曲線

The curve of normalized secant shear modulus G/Gmax versus shear strain plotted on a log scale.G/Gmax ≈ 1 below the linear threshold strain (~10−3 %) and decreases in an S shape as strain increases; in the hyperbolic model \(G/G_{max} = 1/(1+\gamma/\gamma_r)\) = 0.5 at γ = γr.Higher plasticity index and higher confining stress (and OCR for clays) shift the curve to the right, i.e. more linear.With Gmax = ρVs² it gives G at any strain; together with the damping curve it is the soil input of equivalent linear site response analysis.

G/Gmax 對 log γ 的 S 形曲線：小應變時約為 1，應變越大越低（雙曲線在 γ = γr 時為 0.5），同時阻尼上升。PI 與圍壓越大（黏土再加 OCR），曲線越往右、越線性。配合 Gmax 可得任一應變的 G；與阻尼曲線一起是等值線性分析的輸入。

8.5 →8.6 →
⑨Damping ratio curve阻尼曲線

The damping ratio D plotted against log shear strain.It rises from Dmin (about 1 %) at small strains to about 20 % at 1 % strain.It mirrors the modulus reduction curve: the softer the loop, the fatter it is.Each point is usually from a stable loop (about the 10th cycle), so the curve does not describe cycle-by-cycle degradation.

阻尼比對 log γ 作圖，由小應變約 1 % 升到 1 % 應變時約 20 %，與模數折減曲線一降一升。每一點通常取穩定迴圈（約第 10 圈），不含圈數效應。

8.4 →8.5 →
⑩Minimum damping ratio Dmin最小阻尼比

The damping ratio at strains below the linear threshold, about 0.5–1.5 %.It comes from micro-slip at particle contacts and pore-fluid viscosity (rate-dependent), not from the nonlinear shape of the loop.The Masing model gives zero damping at these strains, so Dmin must be added (Darendeli term, or Rayleigh / viscous damping in nonlinear codes).

小應變時量到的阻尼，約 0.5–1.5 %，來自顆粒接觸的微滑動與孔隙流體的黏滯，與迴圈形狀無關。Masing 模型在此趨近 0，要另外加上。

8.5 →8.8 →
⑪Hyperbolic model and reference strain γrHW3雙曲線模型與參考應變

\(\tau = G_{max}\gamma/(1+\gamma/\gamma_r)\), so \(G/G_{max} = 1/(1+\gamma/\gamma_r)\) (Kondner; applied to cyclic loading by Hardin & Drnevich).Reference strain \(\gamma_r = \tau_{max}/G_{max}\); G/Gmax = 0.5 at γ = γr.At γ/γr = 0.1, 1, 10: G/Gmax = 0.91, 0.5, 0.09, so most softening occurs within one log cycle on each side of γr.In practice γr is often fitted to the measured curve rather than computed from strength.

小應變時 τ ≈ Gmaxγ，大應變時趨近 τmax；γ = γr 時 G/Gmax = 0.5。曲線在對數軸上以 γr 為中心對稱。實務上 γr 常當擬合參數，和由強度算出的值不一定相同。

8.6 →
⑫Darendeli (2001) modelDarendeli 修正雙曲線模型

A modified hyperbola with a curvature parameter a: \(G/G_{max} = 1/[1+(\gamma/\gamma_r)^a]\), a = 0.919, based on many resonant column and torsional shear tests.γr (in %) increases with PI, with OCR (only through the PI term) and with σ′m: the curve shifts right.Damping: \(D = b(G/G_{max})^{0.1}D_{Masing} + D_{min}\), which corrects both the small-strain and the large-strain ends of the Masing damping.

加入曲率參數 a，並讓 γr 隨塑性、過壓密與圍壓變化；阻尼用 b 與 (G/Gmax)0.1 縮小大應變段、加上 Dmin 補小應變段。資料多在 0.3 % 以下，更大應變是外插。

8.6 →8.8 →
⑬Equivalent linear and nonlinear models等值線性與非線性模型

Equivalent linear: the soil is described by Gsec(γ) and D(γ); each layer has one G and one D for the whole earthquake, iterated until they are compatible with the layer's strain (SHAKE, Strata; frequency domain).It is linear within each cycle, so loops always close: no permanent strain, no pore pressure, and no strength limit.Nonlinear: backbone curve + unloading–reloading rules; the tangent stiffness changes every time step, and permanent strain (and pore pressure with an effective-stress model) can be computed (DEEPSOIL, OpenSees, FLAC; time domain).

等值線性：每層整場地震一組 G、D，疊代到與應變相容；一圈之內是線性，算不出永久應變與孔隙水壓，也沒有強度上限。非線性：骨幹＋卸載再載入規則，逐步追蹤勁度，可算永久應變（配合有效應力模型可算孔隙水壓）。兩者都需要 Gmax 與 G/Gmax（或骨幹）曲線。

C　看反覆載重

地震的剪應力怎麼換成一個數字、卸載與再載重怎麼畫、迴圈為什麼會一圈比一圈軟。圖中 ⑭–⑱ 對應下表的名詞。

#名詞說明詳細推導
⑭Cyclic shear stress and CSR反覆剪應力與反覆剪應力比

The shear stress on horizontal planes that reverses sign repeatedly during an earthquake, caused mainly by vertically propagating S waves; the soil element is in cyclic simple shear.Seed–Idriss simplified method: \(\tau_{av} = 0.65\,(a_{max}/g)\,\sigma_v\,r_d\); the factor 0.65 turns the peak stress into an equivalent uniform cyclic stress, and rd accounts for the soil column not being rigid.\(CSR = \tau_{av}/\sigma'_v\); a shallow water table increases σv/σ′v and therefore CSR.P waves change the normal stress, but in saturated soil this is carried almost entirely by the pore water, so liquefaction is controlled by shear waves.

地震時水平面上來回變號的剪應力，主要由垂直往上傳的 S 波造成，土壤元素處於反覆單剪狀態。Seed–Idriss 簡化法用 0.65 把峰值換成等值均勻循環，rd 修正土柱並非剛體。地下水位越淺，σv/σ′v 越大、CSR 越高。P 波的正向應力在飽和土中幾乎由孔隙水承擔，所以液化主要由剪力波造成。

8.1 →8.2 →
⑮Masing rulesHW3Masing 準則

Unloading and reloading curves have the shape of the backbone curve enlarged by a factor of 2, with the origin moved to the reversal point (γa, τa): \((\tau-\tau_a)/2 = f\big((\gamma-\gamma_a)/2\big)\).Every reversal starts with slope Gmax; unloading from (γc, τc) ends exactly at (−γc, −τc), so the loop is closed and symmetric about the origin.Hence the loop shape, the G/Gmax curve and the damping curve are all fixed by the backbone.

反轉後的曲線＝骨幹在兩個方向放大 2 倍、原點移到反轉點。每次反轉先回到 Gmax 的硬度再逐漸變軟；放大 2 倍才剛好落到骨幹的對稱點，迴圈閉合且對原點對稱。迴圈、模數與阻尼都由骨幹決定。

8.7 →
⑯Extended Masing rules擴充 Masing 準則

Two additional rules for irregular (earthquake) loading: if an unloading or reloading curve exceeds the previous maximum strain, it follows the backbone curve; if it intersects a previous branch (an inner loop closes), it follows that previous branch.The program must remember every reversal point (a stack of open loops).The HW3 web tool keeps only the last reversal point: identical for 0 → γc → −γc → γc, different for irregular paths.

超過先前最大應變就回到骨幹；與先前分支相交（內迴圈閉合）就改沿那條分支走。程式要記住每個反轉點。HW3 的網頁工具只記最後一個反轉點，規則載重時結果相同，不規則路徑會不同。

8.7 →
⑰Masing dampingHW3Masing 阻尼

The damping ratio of a loop generated by the Masing rules; it comes only from the curvature of the backbone (a straight backbone gives zero area).Hyperbolic backbone: about 2x/(3π) → 0 at small strain, 14.5 % at γ = γr, and → 2/π ≈ 63.7 % at large strain (x = γc/γr).It underestimates small-strain damping (no Dmin) and overestimates large-strain damping (sands usually 20–30 %).HW3 Q2: 4.73 % and 25.3 % at 0.01 % and 0.1 %, versus 3.60 % and 13.16 % measured.

Masing 迴圈的阻尼完全來自骨幹的彎曲。雙曲線骨幹時小應變趨近 0、參考應變處 14.5 %、大應變上限 63.7 %：小應變低估、大應變高估，所以要用 Darendeli 等方法兩端修正。

8.8 →
⑱Degrading and non-degrading behavior退化與非退化

Non-degrading: the loop hardly changes with the number of cycles (dry sand, high-PI clay); the equivalent linear method is suitable.Degrading: saturated loose sand builds up pore pressure every cycle, so each loop is flatter and fatter than the last; the model must be able to generate pore pressure.

非退化：第 10 圈 ≈ 第 1 圈（乾砂、高塑性黏土），等值線性很合適。退化：飽和疏鬆砂每圈孔隙水壓上升，迴圈一圈比一圈平、胖，模型必須能產生孔隙水壓。

簡答題的關鍵觀念

名詞解釋之外，常被問「為什麼」。每題先寫結論，再寫一句理由。

#問題答題重點詳細推導
1Why does G decrease while D increases with strain?為什麼模數降、阻尼升？

Both come from the same loop: as γc increases, more particle contacts slide, the loop lies down (lower Gsec) and opens up (larger area, higher D).Below γtl contacts hardly slide, so G ≈ Gmax and D ≈ Dmin.

同一個迴圈的兩面：應變越大，顆粒接觸滑動越多，迴圈越躺越胖。

8.3 →8.5 →
2Why is soil damping ED/(4πESo) without frequency?為什麼土壤阻尼比不含頻率？

For a viscous damper ED ∝ ω, so ED/(4πESo) = ξ(ω/ωn).Soil damping is frictional (hysteretic); in the earthquake frequency range the loop area depends on strain amplitude, not on frequency.So D is defined as the viscous damping that dissipates the same energy at resonance (ω/ωn = 1); in frequency-domain analysis this becomes G* = G(1 + 2iD).

黏滯阻尼耗能隨頻率增加；土壤是摩擦型遲滯阻尼，迴圈面積只看應變幅度，所以取共振時等值的黏滯阻尼比。

8.4 →
3Which modulus does equivalent linear analysis use?等值線性用哪一個模數？

The secant modulus Gsec = τc/γc, together with D: an ellipse with the correct tip and area replaces the real loop.Gtan at one point is larger than Gsec just after reversal and smaller near the tip, so using it would over- or underestimate stiffness.

用割線模數；橢圓的尖端與面積正確，但反轉初期很硬、尖端很軟的細節被丟掉。

8.3 →
4When can the equivalent linear method not be used?什麼時候不能用等值線性？

When permanent deformation is needed (e.g. displacement behind a retaining wall): loops always close within one cycle, so no permanent strain accumulates.When pore pressure matters (γ > γtv in saturated sand): it is a total stress method without shear–volume coupling.At very large strains, where there is no strength limit in the model. Use a nonlinear (effective-stress) time-domain model instead.

要算永久變形、孔隙水壓，或應變很大時不行；改用非線性時間域（有效應力）模型。HW3 Q3(b) 問的就是這件事。

8.5 →8.6 →
5How do PI and confining stress affect the curves?PI 與圍壓如何影響曲線？

Higher PI or higher σ′m → larger γr → G/Gmax curve shifts right (more linear) and damping is lower at the same strain.Plastic fines have adsorbed water and electro-chemical bonds that resist sliding (Vucetic & Dobry 1991); confining stress matters most for non-plastic soils.OCR affects clays only; number of cycles degrades saturated sand; frequency raises clay stiffness by about 10 % per log cycle.Example: Mexico City 1985: lake clay with PI ≈ 200 stayed nearly linear with low damping and strongly amplified ~2 s motion.

PI、圍壓越大，曲線越右移、阻尼越低。OCR 只影響黏土；圈數使飽和砂退化；頻率使黏土勁度每 log cycle 約增 10 %。1985 墨西哥市的高塑性黏土幾乎保持線性，放大約 2 秒週期的運動。

8.9 →
6Can one MRD curve be used at all depths?同一種砂，淺處深處可用同一條曲線嗎？

No. σ′m increases with depth, so γr increases and the curve shifts right; each layer needs a curve adjusted to its own σ′m.Getting Vs (Gmax) right matters even more, because it sets the absolute stiffness level.

不行，深處 σ′m 大、曲線右移；要依各層 σ′m 調整。先把 Vs 弄對，誤差影響通常大於曲線形狀。

8.9 →
7Why is the backbone enlarged by exactly 2 in the Masing rules?為什麼放大 2 倍？

Only with a factor of 2 does unloading from (γa, τa) end at (−γa, −τa): τ = τa + 2f(−γa) = −τa, so the loop closes on the backbone and is symmetric.The slope at reversal is f′(0) = Gmax: soil is stiff again right after reversal and then softens, as observed in tests.

放大 2 倍才剛好落到骨幹的對稱點，迴圈閉合；反轉瞬間斜率回到 Gmax，與試驗一致。

8.7 →
8Why must Masing damping be corrected at both ends?HW3Masing 阻尼為何兩端都要修正？

Small strain: the backbone is nearly straight, so the loop has almost no area (D → 0), but real soil has Dmin ≈ 1 % from micro-slip and viscosity.Large strain: the reversal curves (backbone × 2) make loops too fat (up to 2/π ≈ 64 %), while real sands stay around 20–30 %.Overestimated damping is unconservative: the soil absorbs too much energy and the predicted surface motion is too small. Zero small-strain damping makes resonance peaks too sharp.Fixes: Darendeli b(G/Gmax)0.1 and Dmin, non-Masing rules, MRDF; always compare the model's damping curve with the target curve.

小應變缺 Dmin（共振峰過尖），大應變迴圈過胖（高估阻尼會低估地表運動，不保守）。修正：Darendeli、非 Masing 規則、MRDF；做非線性分析一定要比較模型阻尼與目標阻尼。

9Does the MRD curve include the number of cycles?MRD 曲線含圈數效應嗎？

No. Each point is Gsec and D of a stable loop (about the 10th cycle) at one strain amplitude.Above γtv the 1st and 50th cycles of saturated sand may differ greatly, which one curve cannot describe.Darendeli data are mostly below 0.3 %; larger strains are extrapolated and are often corrected using the shear strength.

不含。每點是某應變下穩定迴圈的值；超過 γtv 的飽和砂逐圈改變，一條曲線描述不了。Darendeli 0.3 % 以上是外插。

常見陷阱與計算機提醒

  • % 與小數：算 Gsec、τ、ESo 時應變一定用小數（0.1 % = 0.001）；直接代 % 會讓 D 小 100 倍。Darendeli 的 γr 與 γ 用 %，HW3 Masing 工具的 γref = 0.0004 是小數（= 0.04 %）。
  • D 的兩種意思：單元 4 的 D 是放大因子；單元 8 的 D 是阻尼比（= ξ），HW3 Q2 與計算題整理寫作 ξ。
  • Gsec 不是 Gtan：等值線性用原點到尖端的割線斜率。
  • ESo 只是三角形：½τcγc，第一象限原點到尖端；ED 是整個迴圈面積（四個象限）。
  • σ′m 不是 σ′v0：Hardin 與 Darendeli 都用平均有效應力，先用 K0 換算。
  • OCR 乘在 PI 上：乾淨砂（PI = 0）改 OCR，γr 不變。
  • Masing 卸載的 2 倍：引數是 (γ − γa)/2，外面乘 2；雙曲線展開後分母是 1 + |γ − γa|/(2γr)。算完代 γ = −γc 檢查是否得 −τc。
  • Masing 阻尼兩端都錯：小應變 → 0（要加 Dmin），大應變上限 63.7 %（要折減）；HW3 Q2 的 25.3 % 對量測 13.16 %。
  • HW3 工具是簡化版：只記最後反轉點，規則路徑與擴充 Masing rules 相同，不規則路徑會不同。
  • MRD 橫軸要用對數：小應變端 G/Gmax 應接近 1；G 的單位用數量級檢查（kPa 與 MPa 差 1000）。
  • 超過 γtv 不能只靠等值線性：它算不出孔隙水壓。
