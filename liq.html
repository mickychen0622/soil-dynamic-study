符號與基本關係

液化的核心只有一條：不排水 → 孔隙水壓上升 → 有效應力下降 → 強度與勁度下降。所有比值的分母都是有效應力。

\[ \begin{aligned} &\sigma' = \sigma - u,\qquad r_u = \frac{\Delta u}{\sigma'_0} \\ &\sigma' = (1 - r_u)\,\sigma'_0 \end{aligned} \]
符號意義常用單位
\(\Delta u\)Excess pore water pressure 超額孔隙水壓（超過靜水壓的部分）kPa
\(\sigma'_0\)Initial effective confining stress 初始有效應力；三軸用 σ′3c、單剪用 σ′v0kPa
\(\sigma'_{3c}\)Effective consolidation (cell) pressure in the triaxial test 三軸壓密有效圍壓kPa
\(\sigma'_{v0}\)Initial vertical effective stress 初始垂直有效應力kPa
\(r_u\)Excess pore pressure ratio 超額孔隙水壓比—
\(\tau_{cyc}\)Cyclic shear stress amplitude 反覆剪應力振幅（單剪）kPa
\(\sigma_d\)Cyclic deviator stress amplitude 反覆軸差應力振幅（單振幅；講義寫 σdc）kPa
\(CSR_{ss},\ CSR_{tx}\)Cyclic stress ratio in simple shear / triaxial 單剪、三軸的反覆應力比—
\(CRR\)Cyclic resistance ratio 抗液化強度比（指定圈數下造成液化的 CSR）—
\(N,\ N_L\)Number of cycles; cycles to liquefaction 圈數；達液化的圈數圈
\(a,\ b\)Coefficient and exponent of CRR = aN−b 抗液化曲線的係數與指數—
\(c_r\)CSR correction factor, triaxial → simple shear 三軸換單剪的修正因子（講義寫 Cr）—
\(K_0\)Coefficient of lateral earth pressure at rest 靜止土壓力係數—
\(e,\ e_c\)Void ratio; critical void ratio 孔隙比；臨界孔隙比—
\(D_r\)Relative density 相對密度%
\(PI,\ LL,\ w_c\)Plasticity index, liquid limit, water content 塑性指數、液性限度、含水量%
\(I_c\)CPT soil behaviour type index CPT 土壤行為指數—

名詞解釋

作答順序：一句定義 → 公式 → 一兩個關鍵性質。2025 年的格式是「briefly describe in English/Chinese what you know about…」，每題 4 分。前 7 個是易致性，8–13 是狀態與兩種液化，14–19 是室內反覆試驗。

#名詞說明詳細推導
1Soil liquefaction土壤液化

The loss of strength and stiffness of a saturated cohesionless soil when cyclic (earthquake) loading raises the excess pore pressure under undrained conditions.The effective stress drops toward zero: \(\sigma' = (1-r_u)\sigma'_0\).It needs a soil that tends to contract when sheared and loading too fast for the water to drain.

飽和無凝聚性土在反覆載重下，水來不及排出，超額孔隙水壓上升、有效應力趨近零，強度與勁度大幅下降。條件是「土想收縮」加上「水排不掉」。

12.1 →12.3 →
2Liquefaction susceptibility液化易致性

Whether a soil could liquefy at all if the shaking were strong enough.Judged by four criteria, from coarse to fine: historic, geologic, compositional and state criteria.Susceptible does not mean it will liquefy; only susceptible soils go on to a triggering evaluation.

只回答「這種土有沒有可能液化」，用歷史、地質、組成、狀態四個準則由粗到細判斷。易致不等於會液化；不易致的土不必再做觸發評估。

12.1 →
3Historic criteria歷史準則

Based on past earthquake performance: liquefaction case histories and regional liquefaction potential maps.Sites that liquefied before often liquefy again.The maximum distance at which liquefaction has been observed increases with magnitude (Ambraseys 1988): roughly 20 km for M 6, 100 km for M 7 and 300 km for M 8 (read from the chart).

看過去地震的液化案例：同一地點常重複液化；可能液化的最遠距離隨規模增加（Ambraseys 1988 圖讀值：M 6 約 20 km、M 7 約 100 km、M 8 約 300 km）。

12.1 →
4Geologic criteria地質準則

Depositional environment: fluvial, alluvial, colluvial and aeolian deposits, hydraulic fills, mine tailings and uncompacted man-made fills are susceptible (loose, uniform sorting).Hydrological environment: only saturated soil liquefies, so a shallow ground-water table increases susceptibility.Age: Holocene deposits are susceptible; susceptibility decreases with age, and Pleistocene deposits rarely liquefy.

沉積環境（河流、沖積、崩積、風積、水力回填、尾礦、未壓實填土）、地下水位（淺、飽和才會液化）、年代（全新世易液化，越老越不易，更新世很少）。

12.1 →
5Compositional criteria組成準則

Grain size, gradation, grain shape and plasticity.Grain-size bounds: the most liquefiable band is fine to medium sand; a wider band (silt to fine gravel) is potentially liquefiable.Clay resists because of cohesion; clean gravel drains so fast that pore pressure rarely builds up, but gravel can liquefy when drainage is impeded, e.g. under a clay cap (1983 Borah Peak, 2008 Wenchuan).Non-plastic fines are susceptible; uniformly graded and rounded soils are more susceptible than well-graded and angular soils.

粒徑分布落在細砂到中砂的帶內最易液化，外圍較寬的帶（粉土到細礫石）為可能液化。黏土有凝聚力；乾淨礫石排水快，但上覆黏土層阻礙排水時也會液化。非塑性細料易液化；均勻級配比優良級配、圓粒比角粒容易液化。

12.1 →
6Sand-like vs clay-like fine-grained soils細粒土的類砂與類黏土行為

For fine-grained soils, plasticity (PI), not grain size or clay fraction, controls whether they behave like sand.Bray & Sancio (2006): PI ≤ 12 and wc/LL ≥ 0.85 susceptible; 12 < PI ≤ 18 and wc/LL ≥ 0.8 moderately susceptible (test it).Idriss & Boulanger (2008): PI < 7 sand-like. CPT (Robertson & Wride 1998): Ic ≤ 2.6 sand-like.The modified Chinese criteria (clay fraction, LL, wc) are no longer recommended.

細粒土看塑性，不看粒徑或黏粒含量。Bray & Sancio：PI ≤ 12 且 wc/LL ≥ 0.85 易致；12 < PI ≤ 18 且 wc/LL ≥ 0.8 中度，要試驗。Idriss & Boulanger：PI < 7 像砂。CPT：Ic ≤ 2.6 像砂。修正中國準則已不建議使用。

12.1 →
7Cyclic softening反覆軟化

The loss of stiffness and strength of clay-like soils under large cyclic strains.It is not called liquefaction and is not evaluated with the sand CRR curves.

像黏土的土（如 PI ≥ 7）不會「液化」，但大應變下強度與勁度仍會明顯下降，稱反覆軟化，另行評估、不用砂的 CRR 曲線。

12.1 →
8State criteria狀態準則

The initial void ratio (density) and effective confining stress together decide the volumetric tendency during shear.Contractive during shear: susceptible. Dilative during shear: not susceptible (to flow liquefaction).The boundary is the critical state line, so the same sand can be contractive at depth and dilative near the surface.

初始孔隙比與有效圍壓一起決定剪動時想收縮還是膨脹：收縮易致，膨脹不易致。分界是臨界狀態線，所以同一密度的砂在深處可能收縮、淺處可能膨脹。

12.2 →
9Critical void ratio ec臨界孔隙比

Casagrande's drained triaxial tests: dense sand shows a peak then softens and dilates; loose sand hardens and contracts.At large strain both reach the same void ratio, at which shearing continues with no volume change: the critical void ratio.ec decreases as the effective confining stress increases.

排水三軸中密砂先有峰值再軟化、體積膨脹；鬆砂一路硬化、體積收縮；大應變時兩者趨於同一孔隙比（體積不再變化），即臨界孔隙比。圍壓越大，ec 越小。

12.2 →
10Critical (steady) state line, CSL臨界狀態線（穩態線）

The locus of ec versus effective confining stress; nearly a straight line in e–log σ′ space.It is the boundary between contractive (above) and dilative (below) behaviour.It also separates soils susceptible to flow liquefaction (above) from non-susceptible soils (below).

臨界孔隙比對有效圍壓的連線，在 e–log σ′ 上近似直線。線上方收縮、線下方膨脹；也是「會不會流動液化」的分界。

12.2 →
11Drained vs undrained behaviour of loose and dense sand鬆砂與密砂的排水、不排水行為

Drained: σ′ stays constant and the void ratio changes; loose sand contracts and dense sand dilates toward the CSL.Undrained: e stays constant, so the soil reaches the CSL by changing σ′.Loose (contractive) sand: positive Δu, σ′ decreases, strength drops. Dense (dilative) sand: negative Δu, σ′ increases, strength rises.

排水時 σ′ 不變、孔隙比改變（鬆砂往下收縮、密砂往上膨脹）。不排水時 e 不變，只能水平移動到 CSL：鬆砂孔壓上升、有效應力下降（往左），密砂負孔壓、有效應力上升（往右），越剪越強。

12.2 →
12Flow liquefaction流動液化

Occurs when the static shear stress needed for equilibrium exceeds the residual (steady-state) strength of the liquefied soil.Only loose, contractive soils above the CSL are susceptible.Failure is sudden with very large deformations and may continue after shaking stops (e.g. the 1971 Lower San Fernando Dam, tailings-dam flow slides).Less common than cyclic mobility, but the most destructive.

靜態剪應力大於液化後殘餘強度時發生，只發生在 CSL 上方的鬆、收縮性土。破壞突然、變形很大，地震停了還可能繼續流動。較少見但破壞性最大。

12.2 →
13Cyclic mobility★反覆流動性

Occurs when the static shear stress is smaller than the residual strength, but the cyclic shear stress is large enough and lasts enough cycles.Pore pressure and deformation accumulate cycle by cycle; it can occur in loose to dense soils, on either side of the CSL.ru may reach 1 momentarily at stress reversals, but dilation between reversals restores the strength, so strains per cycle are limited.Deformation stops when shaking stops; typical results are lateral spreading, sand boils and settlement.

靜剪應力小於殘餘強度，靠反覆剪應力夠大、圈數夠多，使孔隙水壓與變形逐圈累積；鬆砂到密砂、CSL 上下都可能發生。剪應力反轉瞬間 ru 可達 1，但繼續剪動時膨脹、強度回升，所以每圈變形有限，地震停就停。常見後果是側潰、噴砂、沉陷；日常說的液化多半是這一種。

12.2 →12.3 →
14Excess pore pressure ratio ru超額孔隙水壓比

\(r_u = \Delta u/\sigma'_0\): the excess pore pressure over the initial effective stress (σ′3c in triaxial).It builds up cycle by cycle; strains stay small until ru approaches 1.ru = 1 means zero effective stress (initial liquefaction); in dense sand it occurs only momentarily at stress reversal, giving "banana-shaped" stress paths.With static shear (no stress reversal), ru may never reach 1 although strains become large.

\(r_u = \Delta u/\sigma'_0\)（三軸用 σ′3c），逐圈上升，接近 1 之前應變都很小。ru = 1 表示有效應力為零；密砂只在剪應力反轉的瞬間到 1，應力路徑呈香蕉形。有靜剪應力時 ru 可能到不了 1，但應變仍可很大。

12.3 →
15Cyclic simple shear and cyclic triaxial tests反覆單剪與反覆三軸試驗

Undrained laboratory tests that apply uniform cycles of stress and record Δu and strain until liquefaction.Simple shear: K0-consolidated, cyclic horizontal shear stress τcyc; closer to the field condition of vertically propagating S waves.Triaxial: isotropically consolidated at σ′3c, cyclic deviator stress σd; easy to run but needs the correction factor cr.

不排水、施加等振幅反覆應力，記錄孔隙水壓與應變直到液化。單剪：K0 壓密、施加水平剪應力，接近現地。三軸：等向壓密、施加反覆軸差應力，方便但要用 cr 修正。

12.4 →
16Cyclic stress ratio, CSR (laboratory)反覆應力比（室內）

The cyclic shear stress normalized by the initial effective stress: the loading (demand) applied in a test.Simple shear: \(CSR_{ss} = \tau_{cyc}/\sigma'_{v0}\). Triaxial: \(CSR_{tx} = \sigma_d/(2\sigma'_{3c})\), since the maximum shear stress on the 45° plane is σd/2.

反覆剪應力除以初始有效應力，是試驗施加的載重。三軸的分母是 2σ′3c，因為 45° 面上的最大剪應力是 σd/2。

12.3 →
17Laboratory definition of liquefaction試驗中的液化準則

Initial liquefaction: ru = 1 (Δu = σ′3c).Strain criterion: 5 % double-amplitude axial strain (cyclic triaxial) or 3 % single-amplitude shear strain (simple shear).For loose sand both occur almost together; for dense sand the number of cycles to liquefaction NL can differ markedly, so the criterion must be reported.

兩種定義：初始液化 ru = 1；或應變準則（三軸雙振幅軸應變 5 %、單剪單振幅剪應變 3 %）。鬆砂兩者幾乎同時；密砂差很多，引用 NL 要寫明準則。

12.3 →
18Cyclic resistance ratio CRR and cyclic resistance curve抗液化強度比與抗液化（反覆強度）曲線

CRR is the CSR that causes liquefaction in a specified number of cycles N: the capacity of the soil.Plotting CSR against NL from several tests gives the cyclic strength curve \(CRR = aN^{-b}\), a straight line on log–log axes; b ≈ 0.2–0.35 for clean sand.Denser soil plots higher. A CRR value must state N; the field reference is 15 cycles (M 7.5).The exponent b links cycles of different amplitude.

CRR：在指定圈數下造成液化所需的 CSR，是土的抵抗。多組試驗的 CSR–NL 在雙對數上近似直線 CRR = aN−b，乾淨砂 b ≈ 0.2–0.35；越密曲線越高。說 CRR 一定要說圈數，現地基準 15 圈（M = 7.5）。

12.3 →互動圖 →
19CSR correction factor cr and the two-dimensional effectCSR 修正因子與二維效應

Isotropically consolidated triaxial tests overestimate the resistance of soil in the K0 state: \((CSR)_{ss} = c_r(CSR)_{tx}\).Finn et al. (1971) \(c_r = (1+K_0)/2\); Castro (1975) \(c_r = 2(1+2K_0)/(3\sqrt3)\). For K0 = 0.4, cr ≈ 0.55–0.72; for K0 = 1, 1.0–1.15.Field shaking is multidirectional (two horizontal components), which builds pore pressure faster: \((CSR)_{field} = 0.9(CSR)_{ss} = 0.9c_r(CSR)_{tx}\).

等向壓密三軸高估現地（K0 狀態）的抵抗，用 cr 換成單剪；現地有兩個水平方向的剪應力，孔壓上升更快，再乘 0.9（二維效應）。K0 = 0.4 時 cr 約 0.55–0.72。

B　孔隙水壓與試驗的 CSR

#名稱公式與說明推導／練習

σd 是單振幅；分母的 2 來自 45° 面剪應力 = σd/2。

C　抗液化曲線 CRR = aN−b

#名稱公式與說明推導／練習
C1Cyclic resistance curve抗液化曲線
\[ CRR = aN^{-b},\qquad \log CRR = \log a - b\log N \]

b ≈ 0.2–0.35 for clean sand; the field reference is N = 15 (M 7.5).

雙對數上斜率為 −b 的直線；乾淨砂 b ≈ 0.2–0.35。

12.3 →練習 8.1
C2a and b from two tests由兩組試驗求 a、b
\[ b = \frac{\ln(CRR_1/CRR_2)}{\ln(N_2/N_1)},\qquad a = CRR_1\,N_1^{\,b} \]

Test 1 is the higher CSR (fewer cycles), so both logarithms are positive.

CSR 大的那組圈數少，分子分母都取正值。

12.3 →練習 8.1
C3CRR at another number of cycles換到其他圈數
\[ \frac{CRR_2}{CRR_1} = \left(\frac{N_2}{N_1}\right)^{-b},\qquad N_L = \left(\frac{a}{CSR}\right)^{1/b} \]

The second form gives the cycles to liquefaction under a given CSR.

比值式不需要 a；第二式由給定 CSR 求幾圈液化。

12.3 →練習 8.1
C4Cycles of different amplitude不同振幅的圈數換算
\[ 1\ \text{cycle at}\ \tau_1 \;=\; \left(\frac{\tau_1}{\tau_2}\right)^{1/b}\ \text{cycles at}\ \tau_2 \]

Each cycle uses up 1/NL of the fatigue life; b = 0.25 → one cycle at 1.19τ ≈ 2 cycles at τ.

每圈用掉 1/NL 的壽命。這是後面 Neq 的基礎（不在本頁範圍）。

模擬考 8.1 的數字（對答案用）

σ′3c = 100 kPa；σd = 50 kPa → NL = 12、σd = 36 kPa → NL = 45。CSRtx = 0.25、0.18 → b = 0.249、a = 0.464 → CRRtx(15) = 0.237 → K0 = 0.5、cr = 0.75 → CRRfield = 0.9 × 0.75 × 0.237 = 0.160。完整作答見計算題整理 8.1。

計算題題型與解題步驟

先認出題型，再照步驟寫。每一步都寫「公式 = 代入 = 結果」，CSR、CRR 無單位，應力統一用 kPa。

題型典型已知解題步驟練習
① 細粒土易致性判斷類砂或類黏土PI、LL、wc（或 CPT 的 Ic）、FC
  1. 算 wc/LL
  2. Idriss & Boulanger：PI < 7 → 像砂
  3. Bray & Sancio：PI ≤ 12 且 wc/LL ≥ 0.85 → 易致；12 < PI ≤ 18 且 ≥ 0.8 → 中度，要試驗
  4. 有 CPT：Ic ≤ 2.6 像砂，> 2.6 取樣試驗
  5. 結論寫「易致 → 進入觸發評估」或「像黏土 → 評估反覆軟化」；FC 或黏粒含量本身不是判斷依據
12.1 小測驗
② 狀態與 CSL收縮或膨脹、會不會流動液化e0、σ′0，CSL 圖（或某圍壓下的 ec）
  1. 在 e–log σ′ 圖上點出 (σ′0, e0)，讀同一 σ′0 的 ec
  2. e0 > ec → 收縮：不排水時水平往左，Δu > 0、σ′ 下降
  3. e0 < ec → 膨脹：Δu < 0、σ′ 上升，越剪越強
  4. 收縮且靜剪應力 > 殘餘強度 → 可能流動液化；否則只可能反覆流動性
  5. 同一 e 在較大 σ′ 下更偏收縮（深處的中密砂也可能在線上方）
12.2 圖
③ 孔隙水壓比由量測的 Δu 求 ruσ′3c（或 σ′v0）、各圈 Δu
  1. ru = Δu/σ′0（分母用初始有效應力）
  2. 剩餘有效應力 σ′ = (1 − ru)σ′0；例：σ′3c = 100 kPa、Δu = 65 kPa → ru = 0.65、σ′ = 35 kPa
  3. ru = 1 → 初始液化，記下該圈為 NL（若題目用應變準則，改看 5 % 或 3 %）
12.3
④ 試驗的 CSR模擬考 8.1 (a)σd、σ′3c（三軸）或 τcyc、σ′v0（單剪）
  1. 三軸：CSRtx = σd/(2σ′3c)；例 50/(2 × 100) = 0.25
  2. 單剪：CSRss = τcyc/σ′v0
  3. 反求試驗要施加的載重：σd = 2σ′3c·CSRtx
8.1
⑤ CRR 曲線與圈數模擬考 8.1 (b)(c)兩組以上 (CSR, NL)，或 a、b
  1. 各組 CSR 就是該圈數的 CRR
  2. b = ln(CRR1/CRR2)/ln(N2/N1)，a = CRR1N1b；8.1：b = 0.249、a = 0.464
  3. 指定圈數：CRR = aN−b（或比值式）；8.1：N = 15 → 0.237
  4. 指定 CSR 求圈數：NL = (a/CSR)1/b；8.1 的曲線在 CSR = 0.20 時約 29 圈
  5. 寫明液化準則與圈數

⑥ 三軸換到現地模擬考 8.1 (d)(CSR)tx 或 CRRtx、K0

  3. (CSR)field = 0.9(CSR)ss

簡答題的關鍵觀念

名詞解釋之外，常被問「為什麼」。每題先寫結論，再寫一句理由。

#問題答題重點詳細推導
1Why are loose saturated sands the most susceptible?為什麼疏鬆飽和的砂最危險？

Liquefaction needs a soil that wants to contract and water that cannot escape during shaking.Clay has cohesion and plasticity; clean gravel drains too quickly for pore pressure to build up.Sand and non-plastic silt contract easily yet drain slowly, especially when young, loose, uniformly graded and rounded.

要「想收縮」又「排不掉水」。黏土有凝聚力，乾淨礫石排水太快；砂與非塑性粉土兩個條件都成立，年輕、疏鬆、均勻、圓粒時更明顯。

12.1 →
2Why can the same sand be dense-like near the surface and loose-like at depth?同一種砂為何淺處像密砂、深處像鬆砂？

Whether a soil contracts or dilates depends on its state relative to the CSL, i.e. on both e and σ′.ec decreases as σ′ increases, so a given e lies below the CSL (dilative) at low stress and may lie above it (contractive) at high stress.

收縮或膨脹看的是相對於 CSL 的狀態；圍壓越大 ec 越小，同一孔隙比在深處可能落到線上方而變成收縮。

12.2 →
3Flow liquefaction vs cyclic mobility★流動液化與反覆流動性的差別

Condition: flow liquefaction needs static shear stress > residual strength; cyclic mobility occurs when static shear stress < residual strength.State: flow liquefaction only above the CSL (loose, contractive); cyclic mobility on either side.Deformation: flow is sudden, very large and may continue after shaking; cyclic mobility accumulates incrementally and stops when shaking stops.Examples: flow slides of dams and tailings vs lateral spreading, sand boils and settlement.

條件（靜剪應力 vs 殘餘強度）、狀態（只在 CSL 上方 vs 上下都可）、變形（突然很大、震後持續 vs 逐圈累積、震停即停）、例子（壩體流滑 vs 側潰、噴砂、沉陷）四點對照。

12.2 →
4How does pore pressure build up in an undrained cyclic test?不排水反覆試驗中孔壓怎麼累積？

Each cycle tries to compact the soil; with no drainage, the tendency becomes a rise in Δu and ru increases cycle by cycle.Strains stay small until ru nears 1. Loose sand (Dr = 47 %): strain then runs away in one or two cycles. Dense sand (Dr = 75 %): ru touches 1 at each stress reversal, but dilation between reversals limits the strain per cycle.In the slide example the dense sand needed about three times the shear stress to liquefy in a similar number of cycles.

每圈都想壓密，不排水就變成孔壓上升；ru 接近 1 前應變很小。鬆砂到 1 後應變失控；密砂每次反轉瞬間到 1，但之間膨脹回升，每圈只增加一點應變。講義 p.18 的密砂要約三倍剪應力才在相近圈數液化。

12.3 →
5Why is ru alone not a safe indicator?為什麼只看 ru 不夠？

With an initial static shear stress (sloping ground), the shear stress does not reverse, so ru may never reach 1.Strains can still become very large, so judging by ru = 1 underestimates the hazard; a strain criterion is needed.

有靜剪應力時剪應力不反轉，ru 可能永遠到不了 1，但應變仍可很大；只看 ru 會低估危險。

12.3 →
6Why must a CRR be quoted with N and the criterion?為什麼 CRR 要附圈數與準則？

CRR decreases with the number of cycles (CRR = aN−b), so a value without N has no meaning; the field reference is 15 cycles.For dense sand, NL by ru = 1 and by 5 % strain can differ a lot.

CRR 隨圈數下降，不附圈數沒有意義；密砂用 ru = 1 或 5 % 應變得到的 NL 差很多。

12.3 →
7Why is the triaxial CRR reduced for field use?為什麼三軸 CRR 要折減才能用在現地？

The triaxial specimen is isotropically consolidated, while the field soil is in the K0 state with K0 < 1; the triaxial test overestimates the resistance, corrected by cr < 1.The triaxial test loads in one direction; field shaking has two horizontal components that raise pore pressure faster, giving the factor 0.9.Using the triaxial value directly overestimates field resistance by about 50 % (0.30 vs 0.20 for K0 = 0.5).

等向壓密 vs 現地 K0 狀態（cr）、單向 vs 多方向剪動（0.9）。K0 = 0.5 時三軸 0.30 只剩現地 0.20，直接用會高估約 50 %。

12.4 →
8Why does simple shear approximate the field better than triaxial?為什麼單剪比三軸接近現地？

During an earthquake, vertically propagating S waves apply cyclic horizontal shear on horizontal planes of K0-consolidated soil.Simple shear reproduces this (K0 consolidation, τcyc on the horizontal plane, CSR = τcyc/σ′v0), whereas triaxial applies σd to an isotropic specimen with shear on the 45° plane.

現地是向上傳的剪力波在水平面上施加反覆剪應力、土在 K0 狀態；單剪正是如此，三軸則是等向壓密、剪應力在 45° 面上。

12.4 →
9What factors influence cyclic resistance, and why are in-situ tests preferred?影響抗液化強度的因素，為何實務用現地試驗？

Resistance increases with initial density and confining stress condition, OCR, K0, aging time, favourable soil structure (fabric) and prior seismic straining (small pre-shaking).Soil structure and aging effects are destroyed by sampling; careful freezing and coring preserve them but are expensive.So practice relies on in-situ test results correlated with field case histories.

密度與初始應力狀態、OCR、K0、老化時間、土壤結構、曾受小震預剪都提高 CRR。結構與老化在取樣時就被破壞，冷凍取樣可保留但昂貴，所以實務多用現地試驗配合案例資料。

12.4 →

常見陷阱與計算機提醒

  • 易致不等於會液化：易致性只說「可能」，會不會液化要看地震的 CSR 與土的 CRR。
  • 細粒土看塑性：判斷依據是 PI（與 wc/LL、Ic），不是細粒料比例或黏粒含量；修正中國準則已不建議。
  • 反覆流動性不只發生在密砂：鬆砂到密砂、CSL 上下都可能；流動液化才只限 CSL 上方。
  • 講義 p.13 的縱軸：CSL 圖縱軸標成 Deviator stress，應讀作孔隙比 e（與 p.14、p.15 同一條線）。
  • 分母一律是有效應力：ru 用初始有效應力 σ′0，CSR 用 σ′v0 或 σ′3c，不是總應力。
  • CSRtx 的 2：σd/(2σ′3c)，σd 是單振幅；忘了 2 會讓 CSR 加倍。
  • b 的符號與順序：b = ln(CRR1/CRR2)/ln(N2/N1)，b 為正；CRR = aN−b 的負號已在指數裡。
  • CRR 不寫圈數或準則：密砂 ru = 1 與 5 % 應變的 NL 可以差很多。
  • 只看 ru：有靜剪應力時 ru 到不了 1，應變仍可很大。
  • 三軸 CRR 直接當現地：要乘 cr 與 0.9（兩者都 < 1，是乘不是除）；cr 寫明用 Finn 或 Castro，K0 = 1 時 Castro 給 1.15。
