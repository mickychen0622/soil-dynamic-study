符號與基本關係

水平成層土中垂直傳播的 SH 波，每一層是「上行波＋下行波」。深度 \(z\) 由地表往下為正。

\[ \begin{aligned} &u(z,t) = A\,e^{i(\omega t + kz)} + B\,e^{i(\omega t - kz)} \\ &k = \frac{\omega}{V_s} \end{aligned} \]

\(H,\ h_i\)Soil layer thickness, sublayer thickness 土層厚度、子層厚度m
\(V_s,\ V_r\)Shear wave velocity of soil, of rock 土層、岩盤剪力波速m/s
\(\rho_s,\ \rho_r\)Mass density of soil, of rock 土層、岩盤密度（\(\rho = \gamma/g\)）t/m³
\(G,\ G_{max}\)Shear modulus, 小應變剪力模數 \(G_{max} = \rho V_s^2\)MPa
\(\xi\)Damping ratio 阻尼比（單元 10、11 寫 ξ；HW3、單元 8–9、國考題寫 D）—
\(G^*,\ V_s^*,\ k^*\)Complex modulus, velocity, wave number 複數模數、波速、波數—

名詞解釋

#名詞說明詳細推導
1Ground (site) response analysis地盤反應分析

Computes the motion within and on top of a soil deposit from a known bedrock motion; it models the "site" part of source – path – site.Outputs: surface acceleration history and response spectrum, and the profiles τmax(z) and γmax(z) used in liquefaction and foundation design.Required for soft or special sites, where the code asks for a site-specific analysis.

已知岩盤運動，求土層內部與地表的運動，處理「震源–路徑–場址」中的場址段。輸出地表歷時與反應譜，以及液化評估、基礎設計要用的 τmax(z)、γmax(z)。軟弱或特殊場址規範要求做場址特定分析。

10.1 →
2One-dimensional (1-D) SH-wave assumption一維垂直傳播 SH 波假設

Soil layers are horizontal and the motion is a horizontally polarized shear wave travelling vertically.Justified by Snell's law \(\sin i_1/V_{s1} = \sin i_2/V_{s2}\): Vs decreases upward, so rays bend toward the vertical (60° in 2000 m/s rock → about 5° in a 200 m/s layer).It fails at basin edges, ridges, dams and rapidly varying layers, where 2-D or 3-D models are needed.

假設土層水平、運動是垂直傳播的 SH 波。依 Snell 定律，越往上 Vs 越小，射線一層層被扳直（岩盤中 60°，到 200 m/s 的土層只剩約 5°）。盆地邊緣、山脊、土壩、層厚急變處不適用，要用二維、三維模型。

10.1 →11.4 →
3Outcrop motion vs within motion露頭運動與土層內運動

Outcrop motion: on a free rock surface the shear stress is zero, the reflected wave equals the incident wave, so u = 2A.Within motion: at the soil–rock interface under a soil column, u = A + B, where the downgoing wave B has been changed by the soil layer.Surface and rock-outcrop records are input as outcrop (the program halves them to get A); downhole records are input as within (no doubling).

露頭是自由面，反射波與入射波一樣大，位移 2A；土層內（within）是上行 A 加上已穿過土層、在地表反射回來的下行 B。地表與露頭紀錄設 outcrop（程式除以 2 當入射波），井下紀錄設 within（不乘 2）。

10.2 →
4Transfer function轉換函數

The ratio of an output motion (e.g. ground surface) to an input motion (e.g. bedrock) at each frequency, F(ω).It is complex: its modulus gives the amplification and its phase gives the time delay.Undamped layer on rigid rock: \(F_1 = 1/\cos(\omega H/V_s)\).Its definition must match the input: surface/outcrop (F3) for outcrop input, surface/rock-top (F2) for within input.

每個頻率下輸出運動（如地表）與輸入運動（如岩盤）之比。它是複數：大小是放大倍率、相位是時間延遲。定義必須和輸入運動的種類（outcrop 或 within）一致。

10.3 →10.7 →
5Fundamental site period TsHW2場址基本週期

The first-mode period of a soil layer on bedrock: \(T_s = 4H/V_s\); the layer thickness equals a quarter wavelength (quarter-wavelength resonance).Higher natural frequencies: \(f_n = (2n-1)V_s/4H\), i.e. 1, 3, 5 … times f0.Layered profile: \(T_s \approx 4\sum h_i/V_{si}\) (travel-time estimate).Thick soft deposits have long Ts (Mexico City clay: 4 × 37.5/75 = 2.0 s); in strong shaking Vs drops and Ts lengthens.

土層第一振態的週期 Ts = 4H/Vs，土層厚度恰為四分之一波長。高階頻率是 f0 的 1、3、5 倍。層狀剖面用走時估計。厚而軟的土層週期長（墨西哥市 2.0 s，與 10–20 層樓相近）；強震時 Vs 下降、週期變長。

10.3 →10.6 →
6Mode shapes of a soil layer土層振態

At the n-th resonance the displacement profile is \(\cos k_nz\), with \(k_nH = (2n-1)\pi/2\): zero at the rigid base, maximum at the surface.Mode 2 has a node at z/H = 1/3; mode 3 has nodes at z/H = 0.2 and 0.6.Shear strain \(\gamma = \partial u/\partial z \propto \sin k_nz\) is zero at the surface and largest at the nodes and the base, so higher modes can cause large strains inside the layer.

第 n 振態形狀 cos knz：剛性岩盤處為零、地表最大。第 2 振態節點在 z/H = 1/3，第 3 振態在 0.2、0.6。剪應變是位移的斜率，地表為零、節點與底部最大，所以高振態雖然地表放大小，層內仍可能有大應變。

10.6 →
7Complex shear modulus複數剪力模數

\(G^* = G(1+2i\xi)\): represents frequency-independent (hysteretic) soil damping in a frequency-domain analysis.It comes from a Kelvin–Voigt solid with viscosity η = 2Gξ/ω, so the damping does not grow with frequency.Then \(V_s^* \approx V_s(1+i\xi)\) and \(k^* \approx k(1-i\xi)\); the τ–γ loop is an ellipse with the same area (energy loss per cycle) as the real loop.

在頻率域表示與頻率無關的遲滯阻尼：Kelvin–Voigt 的黏滯係數取 η = 2Gξ/ω，頻率消去。應力應變迴圈變成橢圓，形狀與真實迴圈不同，但面積（每圈消散能量）相同。與 SDOF 的複數勁度 k(1+2iξ) 同一個想法。

10.5 →4.8 →
8Impedance ratio and radiation dampingHW2阻抗比與輻射阻尼

Impedance ratio \(\alpha_z = \rho_sV_s/(\rho_rV_r)\); typical soil on rock 0.1–0.3.Radiation damping: on elastic (not rigid) rock, part of the downgoing wave passes into the rock and never returns, carrying energy out of the layer.Even undamped soil then has a finite peak 1/αz; with damping the first peak ≈ \(1/(\alpha_z + \xi\pi/2)\), i.e. an equivalent \(\xi_{rad} = 2\alpha_z/\pi\).αz changes the peak height, not the peak frequency.

阻抗比越小（軟土坐在硬岩上），能量越被困在土層內、共振越強。彈性岩盤讓下行波離開土層，帶走能量，等於額外的阻尼；無材料阻尼時峰值也只有 1/αz。峰的位置不變，4H/Vs 仍成立。

10.7 →
9Frequency-domain linear analysis (FFT procedure)頻率域線性分析流程

FFT the input history → multiply each frequency component by the complex transfer function → inverse FFT to get the output history.Valid only because the system is linear (superposition).Practical points: pad zeros to at least twice the record length; the highest usable frequency is the Nyquist frequency 1/(2Δt); keep the phase of F.

輸入歷時做 FFT，逐頻率乘上複數轉換函數，再逆 FFT。能逐頻率相乘的唯一理由是系統線性、疊加原理成立。補零到兩倍以上避免尾端繞回；最高頻率為 Nyquist 頻率；只乘 |F| 會讓波形錯誤。

10.4 →
10Layered recursion (SHAKE)層狀土的逐層遞迴

At each interface, displacement and shear stress continuity give Am+1, Bm+1 from Am, Bm (a 2 × 2 transfer matrix per layer).Start at the free surface with A1 = B1 = 1 and march down to the rock; any depth-to-depth transfer function, and the strain transfer function, follows.It is the core of SHAKE (Schnabel, Lysmer & Seed 1972) and of the equivalent-linear mode of Strata and DEEPSOIL.

每個界面用位移連續、應力連續兩個條件，由上一層的 A、B 推出下一層，從地表 A1 = B1 = 1 一路推到岩盤，不必解大型聯立方程。對深度微分可得應變轉換函數，等值線性每次疊代都要用。

10.8 →
11Equivalent linear analysisHW3等值線性分析

Linear analyses repeated with G and ξ compatible with the effective strain in each layer until convergence, as in SHAKE.Start with Gmax, ξmin → compute γmax → γeff = Rγγmax → read new G, ξ from the curves → repeat until the change is within about 5 % (usually 5–8 iterations).G and ξ stay constant during the whole record, so it is still a linear, frequency-domain, total stress method.It cannot compute pore pressure or permanent deformation, and is unreliable above γmax ≈ 0.5–1 %.

每層用與有效應變相容的 G、ξ 做線性分析，反覆疊代到收斂。整段歷時用同一組性質，仍是線性的頻率域總應力分析；算不出孔隙水壓與永久變形，最大應變超過約 0.5–1 % 不可靠。HW3 Q3 用它求第 3 層在 γeff = 0.05 % 的 G 與阻尼。

11.1 →11.3 →
12Effective shear strainHW3有效剪應變

The representative strain used to read the G/Gmax and damping curves: \(\gamma_{eff} = R_\gamma\gamma_{max}\), \(R_\gamma = (M-1)/10\) (Idriss & Sun, SHAKE91); 0.65 for M 7.5 is a common default.γmax occurs only for an instant; reading the curves at γmax would overestimate the nonlinearity.Larger M → longer shaking → more cycles near γmax → larger Rγ.

讀曲線用的代表應變。γmax 只出現一瞬間，直接用會高估非線性。規模越大、延時越長，Rγ 越大（M 7.5 → 0.65、6.5 → 0.55）。報告的應變剖面仍畫 γmax，不乘 Rγ。

11.1 →
13Implied shear strength隱含剪力強度

A G/Gmax curve together with Gmax implies a full stress–strain curve: \(\tau = (G/G_{max})\,G_{max}\,\gamma\).At large strain this stress may exceed the shear strength (Su for clay, σ′v tan φ′ for sand), which is physically impossible.Check every layer with γmax > 0.1 %, and use strength-adjusted curves when needed.

曲線配上 Gmax 就隱含一條應力應變曲線，大應變時可能超過土壤強度。例：NC 黏土 Gmax = 20 MPa、1 % 時 G/Gmax = 0.15 → τ = 30 kPa，超過 Su = 25 kPa，要改用強度修正曲線。軟黏土最常出問題。

11.2 →
14Nonlinear (time-domain) analysis非線性（時間域）分析

The equation of motion is integrated step by step in the time domain; the stiffness of each sublayer is updated at every step with a backbone curve and the Masing rules.Explicit finite differences need Δt < Δz/Vs; an elastic base is modelled by a dashpot ρrVr (transmitting boundary).It can model large strain and permanent deformation, and liquefaction when coupled with a pore pressure model.It is not automatically better: it is only as good as its calibration and its small-strain viscous damping.

在時間域逐步積分，每一步依當下的應力、應變更新勁度（骨架曲線＋Masing 規則），強震段軟、尾段再變硬。可算大應變、永久變形，配合孔隙水壓模型可做液化。參數多，小應變黏滯阻尼怎麼加是重要的不確定來源。

11.3 →
15Total stress vs effective stress analysisHW3總應力分析與有效應力分析

Total stress analysis: uses G and ξ without computing pore pressures (e.g. equivalent linear analysis).Effective stress analysis: couples a pore pressure model, so it predicts excess pore pressure, softening, liquefaction and permanent deformation.Saturated sand strained above the volumetric threshold γtv (≈ 0.01 %) needs the latter, or the Ch8 liquefaction procedures.

總應力分析只用 G、ξ，不算孔隙水壓；有效應力分析結合孔隙水壓模型。HW3 Q3：第 3 層飽和砂 γeff = 0.05 % > γtv，孔隙水壓會逐圈累積，等值線性預測不了。

11.3 →8.5 →
16Deconvolution反摺積

Running a linear analysis backwards: divide a surface record by the transfer function to obtain the rock (or any depth) motion.Used to remove the site effect from a surface record before using it as rock input elsewhere.Only possible where F is not close to zero (otherwise noise is amplified), and not possible for a truly nonlinear analysis.

把地表紀錄除以轉換函數，反推岩盤運動。台灣強震站多在地表，軟土站的紀錄已含場址效應，要先反摺積才能當另一場址的岩盤輸入。轉換函數接近零的頻率不可行；真正的非線性分析不能倒推。

11.1 →10.2 →
172-D and 3-D dynamic analysis二維與三維動力分析

Needed when the site is not horizontally layered or contains structures: earth dams, slopes, retaining walls, tunnels, basin edges (plane strain for long sections).3-D for strongly varying ground in all directions, dams in narrow canyons, and strong soil–structure interaction.Finite elements with Rayleigh damping \([C] = a_0[M] + a_1[K]\); element size < λmin/5–8; absorbing boundaries; equivalent-linear iteration is done element by element, not layer by layer.

不是水平成層、或土中有結構物時要用：土壩、邊坡、擋土牆、隧道、盆地邊緣（長斷面用平面應變）。窄峽谷中的土壩、強烈土壤–結構互制要三維。元素尺寸小於最短波長的 1/5–1/8；邊界要能吸收反射波；等值線性逐元素疊代。

11.4 →
18Shear-beam model and whiplash effect剪力梁法與鞭梢效應

An earth dam is cut into horizontal slices that deform only in horizontal shear, giving a 1-D shear beam whose width varies with height.Triangular dam (Gazetas 1982): \(T_1 = 2.61H/V_s\), shorter than 4H/Vs of a uniform layer because the section is wider at the base.Whiplash: the narrow crest has little mass, so the 2nd and 3rd modes strongly amplify motion near the crest.

把土壩切成水平薄片，變成寬度隨高度變化的一維剪力梁。三角形壩 T1 = 2.61H/Vs（50 m、300 m/s → 0.435 s）。壩頂窄、質量小，高振態在壩頂劇烈放大，像鞭梢一樣；壩頂常見縱向裂縫。

簡答題的關鍵觀念

名詞解釋之外，常被問「為什麼」。每題先寫結論，再寫一句理由。

#問題答題重點詳細推導
1What goes wrong if outcrop and within are confused?outcrop 與 within 設錯會怎樣？

A within (downhole) record set as outcrop is halved and a downgoing wave is added again: the effect of the soil layer is counted twice.An outcrop record set as within assumes A + B = 2A, i.e. it ignores how the layer changes the downgoing wave.The difference is largest near the layer's resonant frequency, where the within motion has a dip.

井下紀錄設成 outcrop，土層效應被算兩次；露頭紀錄設成 within，忽略土層對下行波的作用。共振頻率附近差最多，地表結果可差兩倍以上。

10.2 →
2Why is site amplification finite even without soil damping?土沒有阻尼，放大為什麼仍有限？

Real rock is not rigid: part of each downgoing wave is transmitted into the rock and never comes back (radiation damping).The peak is then 1/αz; with material damping ≈ 1/(αz + ξπ/2).

彈性岩盤讓能量往下離開，峰值上限 1/αz。輻射阻尼（αz ≈ 0.1–0.3）常比材料阻尼 ξπ/2（5 % 時 0.08）更重要。

10.7 →
3Rigid base or elastic base?剛性基盤還是彈性基盤？

A rigid base removes radiation damping and overestimates resonant amplification; use it only with a within motion recorded at that depth.With an outcrop (or surface rock) record, use an elastic base.The peak frequencies hardly change; only the peak heights do (540 ft example: 12.8 → 3.2).

剛性基盤能量出不去，會高估共振放大，只配 within 輸入；露頭紀錄配彈性基盤。換基盤只改峰高、不改峰的位置。

10.7 →
4Why are higher modes amplified less?為什麼高振態放大較小？

The damped peak is 1/(ξknH); knH grows as π/2, 3π/2, 5π/2, so the peaks drop as 1, 1/3, 1/5.Hysteretic damping removes a fixed fraction of energy per cycle, and high frequencies go through more cycles while crossing the layer.So a soil layer acts as a low-pass filter; with large ξ it even de-amplifies high frequencies (|F| < 1).

峰值 1/(ξkH)，kH 越大越低；高頻穿過土層經歷較多圈，被阻尼吃掉較多。土層像低通濾波器，阻尼大時高頻甚至被削弱。

10.5 →
5Why must the transfer function be complex?為什麼轉換函數要保留複數？

The phase of F is the time delay of each frequency through the layer.Multiplying by |F| only gives the right Fourier amplitudes but a wrong waveform, arrival time and peak value.

相位代表各頻率穿過土層的延遲；只乘 |F|，振幅譜一樣，但歷時形狀與峰值都錯。

10.4 →
6Soft site or stiff site: which is more dangerous?軟弱場址與堅硬場址哪個危險？

It depends on the building period: the danger is greatest when the building period is close to the site period.A soft basin (Ts ≈ 1 s) mainly amplifies long periods; a stiff site (Ts ≈ 0.2 s) short periods. A 10-story building (≈ 1 s) is worst on the soft basin.PGA alone is misleading: deep soft soils change the long-period spectrum, velocity and displacement much more than PGA.

要先問建築週期；建築週期接近場址週期最危險。軟弱盆地放大長週期、堅硬場址放大短週期。只看 PGA 不夠，深厚軟土改變最多的是長週期譜值、速度與位移（1985 墨西哥市）。

7How does site response change in strong shaking?強震時場址反應怎麼變？

Larger strain → G decreases (Vs down, Ts longer) and ξ increases (peaks lower); both reduce high-frequency amplification.Example: 30 m, Vs 200 → 150 m/s, ξ 5 → 10 %: f0 1.67 → 1.25 Hz, first peak 12.8 → 6.4.The amplification ratio drops as input intensity rises; whether the surface PGA is de-amplified depends on the input frequency content.

應變大 → G 降、ξ 升 → 場址週期變長、放大變小。土壤不是彈簧：輸入加倍，地表增加不到兩倍。同一場址小地震量到的週期短、大地震長，是非線性最直接的證據。

8Why is the travel-time period only approximate?為什麼層狀剖面的走時法只是近似？

4Σh/Vs treats the profile as one uniform layer with the same travel time; reflections at internal interfaces are ignored.A soft layer between stiff layers (velocity inversion) creates extra resonant peaks with irregular spacing and heights.

走時法把剖面當成同走時的均勻層，忽略層間反射；速度反轉的夾層會產生額外共振峰。540 ft 例：走時法 0.70 Hz，遞迴 0.90 Hz。

9Why use the effective strain rather than γmax?HW3為什麼用有效應變讀曲線？

One set of G, ξ must represent the whole record; γmax occurs only once and most cycles are smaller.Reading the curves at γmax would make the soil too soft and too damped; Rγ = 0.5–0.7 (larger for longer, bigger earthquakes).

一組 G、ξ 要代表整段地震；γmax 只出現一次，用它會高估非線性。Rγ 是本方法較主觀的參數之一；Ch8 簡化液化法的 0.65 是同一想法。

10Limitations of equivalent linear analysis; when is nonlinear analysis needed?等值線性的限制，何時要非線性？

High frequencies are over-damped, because the large damping set by low-frequency strains is applied to all frequencies.Unreliable above γmax ≈ 0.5–1 %: a very soft layer acts as an isolator and the surface acceleration is underestimated.Total stress and 1-D: no pore pressure, no permanent deformation, no topographic or basin effects.Use nonlinear (effective stress) analysis for large strain, possible liquefaction or permanent deformation, and compare with the equivalent-linear result.

高頻過度阻尼；大應變不可靠；總應力、一維。應變超過約 0.5 %、可能液化或需要永久變形時加做非線性分析，並以等值線性為基準：中等應變時兩者應接近。

11Can equivalent linear analysis predict excess pore pressure?HW3等值線性能預測超額孔隙水壓嗎？

No. It is a total stress analysis with fixed G and ξ, no shear–volume coupling and no tracking of effective stress.In saturated sand above γtv ≈ 0.01 %, pore pressure builds up cycle by cycle and G keeps dropping, which one set of equivalent properties cannot represent.Use its cyclic shear stresses in the Ch8 liquefaction methods, or run an effective stress nonlinear analysis.

不能。總應力、固定 G 與 ξ、無剪力–體積耦合。HW3 Q3 第 3 層 0.05 % > γtv，孔隙水壓會累積；只能把算出的反覆剪應力代入 Ch8 的液化評估，或做有效應力分析。

12Why are thin sublayers and a strength check needed?為什麼要切薄子層、檢查隱含強度？

Each sublayer has one effective strain and one set of G, ξ, so it must be thin enough to follow strain and confining pressure with depth, and to carry the shortest wavelength (h ≤ λmin/5).A modulus curve with Gmax implies a stress–strain curve that may exceed the strength; a too-strong weak layer transmits too much shear and overestimates surface acceleration.

每個子層只有一組性質，要薄到跟得上應變與圍壓的變化，並能表示最短波長。曲線隱含的應力不可超過強度，否則弱層傳太多剪力、高估地表加速度。

13When is a 2-D or 3-D analysis needed?何時需要二維、三維分析？

2-D: the cross-section is not horizontally layered or contains structures (dams, slopes, walls, tunnels, basin edges).3-D: soil varies strongly in all directions, dams in narrow canyons, strong soil–structure interaction (adjacent tall buildings, underground stations).Check: mesh fine enough (< λmin/5–8), boundaries far enough (double the width and rerun), sensible Rayleigh damping frequencies.

斷面不是水平成層或有結構物用二維；三方向都變化、窄峽谷土壩、強烈互制用三維。檢查網格、邊界（寬度加倍結果不變）與 Rayleigh 阻尼頻率的選擇。

常見陷阱與計算機提醒

  • kH 用弧度：kH = 2πfH/Vs，cos 要在 RAD 模式算；kH 無因次。
  • f 與 ω：k = ω/Vs 用的是 ω = 2πf，不是 f。
  • fn 的兩種寫法：(2n−1)Vs/4H（n 從 1）與 (2n+1)Vs/4H（n 從 0）相同，不要混用 n 的起點；高階頻率是 1、3、5 倍，不是 1、2、3 倍。
  • 走時平均，不是算術平均：層狀剖面 Ts ≈ 4Σhi/Vsi；用厚度加權的算術平均會高估 Vs、低估週期。
  • 岩盤以下不要加：只加岩盤頂以上的層，岩盤定義看題目（N.7 是 Vs > 360 m/s，所以只算到 27 m）。
  • outcrop 與 within：地表或露頭紀錄是 outcrop（除以 2 當入射波）；井下紀錄是 within，不乘 2。
  • rigid base 高估共振：剛性基盤移除輻射阻尼，只適合 within 輸入。
  • 只乘 |F|：轉換函數要保留複數，否則歷時形狀錯誤。
  • 只看 PGA：深厚軟弱土層改變最多的是長週期譜值、速度與位移。
  • G 乘的是現地 Gmax：HW3 Q3 要用下孔試驗的 Gmax（140.9 MPa），不是共振柱的 93.2 MPa。
  • 報告的應變：最大應變剖面畫 γmax，不乘 Rγ；Rγ 只用來讀曲線。
  • 阻尼比的符號：單元 10、11 寫 ξ；HW3、單元 8–9 與國考題寫 D；講義圖中的 β 也是阻尼比。代入公式時用小數（9.8 % → 0.098）。
  • 講義 F3 圖的標籤：圖上峰值約 1/√標籤，行為像 αz²；理論峰值以 1/αz 為準。
  • 非線性不等於比較準：它只和率定與小應變黏滯阻尼一樣好；先做等值線性當基準。
