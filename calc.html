Q2

Machine foundation SDOF: ωn, ξ, ωd, δ機器基礎 SDOF

2025 Midterm · 20 pts
A foundation–machine system is modeled as an SDOF system with W = 1000 kN, k = 200,000 kN/m and c = 2340 kN·s/m. Determine (a) the natural circular frequency ωn, (b) the damping ratio ξ, (c) the damped circular frequency ωd and (d) the logarithmic decrement δ (5 pts each).基礎加機器重 1000 kN、彈簧 200,000 kN/m、阻尼器 2340 kN·s/m，求自然圓頻率、阻尼比、阻尼圓頻率、對數遞減。
Solution · 解題步驟與答案
  1. Convert weight to mass: \(m = W/g = 1000/9.81 = 101.94\) t (kN divided by m/s² gives t).重量除以 g 換成質量。
  2. (a) \(\omega_n = \sqrt{k/m} = \sqrt{200{,}000/101.94} = 44.29\) rad/s (\(f_n\) = 7.05 Hz).自然圓頻率。
  3. (b) \(c_c = 2m\omega_n = 2(101.94)(44.29) = 9030\) kN·s/m, so \(\xi = c/c_c = 2340/9030 = 0.259\).先求臨界阻尼係數，再求阻尼比。
  4. (c) \(\omega_d = \omega_n\sqrt{1-\xi^2} = 44.29\sqrt{1-0.259^2} = 42.78\) rad/s.阻尼圓頻率。
  5. (d) \(\delta = 2\pi\xi/\sqrt{1-\xi^2} = 1.686\). With ξ = 0.26 the approximation \(2\pi\xi\) = 1.628 is about 3 % low, so use the exact form.ξ 不小，近似式低估約 3 %，寫精確式。

Answer: ωn = 44.3 rad/s; ξ = 0.259; ωd = 42.8 rad/s; δ = 1.69.

Q3

Undamped free vibration: phase angle and amplitude無阻尼自由振動

2025 Midterm · 10 pts
For the same system, the initial displacement and velocity are u(0) = 0.05 m and u̇(0) = 0.01 m/s. Ignoring damping, determine (a) the phase angle of the displacement and (b) the displacement amplitude (5 pts each).同一系統，初始位移 0.05 m、初速 0.01 m/s，不計阻尼，求相位角與位移振幅。
Solution · 解題步驟與答案
  1. Undamped free vibration: \(u(t) = u_0\cos\omega_nt + \dfrac{\dot u_0}{\omega_n}\sin\omega_nt\), with \(\omega_n\) = 44.29 rad/s from Q2; \(\dot u_0/\omega_n = 0.01/44.29 = 2.258\times10^{-4}\) m.無阻尼自由振動的通解，ωn 沿用 Q2。
  2. (b) Amplitude \(\rho = \sqrt{u_0^2 + (\dot u_0/\omega_n)^2} = 0.0500\) m. The initial velocity is small, so the amplitude is almost equal to the initial displacement.初速很小，振幅幾乎等於初始位移。
  3. (a) Writing \(u = \rho\sin(\omega_nt + \theta)\): \(\tan\theta = u_0\omega_n/\dot u_0 = 221.5\), θ = 89.74° (1.566 rad). Writing \(u = \rho\cos(\omega_nt - \alpha)\): \(\tan\alpha = \dot u_0/(u_0\omega_n)\), α = 0.26°. Both describe the same motion; state which form you use.sin 形式與 cos 形式的相位角不同，作答時寫清楚採用哪一種。

Answer: amplitude ρ ≈ 0.0500 m; phase angle θ = 89.7° (sine form; 0.26° in cosine form).

HW1

Strong-motion records: integration, spectrum and parameters強地動紀錄

Homework
Part A: record washing-machine acceleration with phyphox and obtain the velocity, displacement and predominant frequency. Part B: for 30 Chi-Chi PEER records, compute the RotD100 PGA, PGV, PGD, CAV, AI, Tm and Ds, and plot them against PGA and Rrup.Part A 手機量洗衣機振動；Part B 集集地震 30 筆紀錄的地動參數。

Vs30: two averages and three site classification systemsVs30 與場址分類

HW3 Q2

Masing loops and damping ratioMasing 迴圈與阻尼比

Homework · key
Hyperbolic backbone with Gmax = 94,000 kPa and γref = 0.0004 (decimal); loading path 0 → γc → −γc → γc. (a) Loops and D = ED/(4πESo) for γc = 0.01 % and 0.1 %, compared with Q1. (b) Why the Masing damping differs from the measured values at very small and at large strains.雙曲線骨幹的 Masing 迴圈，由面積求阻尼比並與量測比較；說明兩端為何不同。
Solution · 解題步驟與答案
  1. Use decimal strain: \(\tau_c = G_{max}\gamma_c/(1+\gamma_c/\gamma_{ref})\). 0.01 % → \(94{,}000\times0.0001/1.25 = 7.52\) kPa; 0.1 % → \(94{,}000\times0.001/3.5 = 26.86\) kPa.應變一律用小數。
  2. Unloading branch \(\tau = \tau_c + 2f\!\left(\frac{\gamma-\gamma_c}{2}\right)\); integrate the loop area ED numerically; \(E_{So} = \frac12\tau_c\gamma_c\).卸載分支是骨幹放大 2 倍；迴圈面積數值積分。
  3. 0.01 %: ED = 0.223, ESo = 0.376 J/m³, D = 4.73 % (measured 3.60 %). 0.1 %: ED = 42.64, ESo = 13.43 J/m³, D = 25.3 % (measured 13.2 %).Masing 阻尼都高於量測值。
  4. (b) Small strain: the backbone is nearly linear, unloading and reloading coincide and the Masing damping tends to zero, but tests still show about 1 % (Dmin, from micro-slip and viscosity). Large strain: Masing assumes the reversal curves are the backbone scaled by 2, so the loops are too fat and damping is overestimated. Darendeli corrects both ends with \(b(G/G_{max})^{0.1}D_{Masing} + D_{min}\).小應變少了 Dmin，大應變迴圈太胖；Darendeli 兩端都修正。

Answer: Masing damping 4.73 % and 25.3 %, i.e. 1.3 and 1.9 times the measured values, while the moduli agree (0.800 vs 0.799).

Homework · key
Three sand layers (0–4, 4–10, 10–20 m), water table at 1 m, ρ = 1.95 t/m³, K0 = 0.5. Downhole S-wave arrivals at 2, 4, 7, 10, 15, 20 m: 13.5, 27.2, 41.3, 55.3, 74.2, 92.5 ms. HW3 Q4

Isolation pad SDOF: damping, transmissibility and energy隔振墊 SDOF

Homework · key
An instrument (m = 2000 kg) rests on an isolation pad; a shaker applies p0 = 0.5 kN and the steady-state displacement is measured from 4 to 12 Hz. (a) fn, k, ρs, ξ by the resonance amplification and half-power methods, and c. (b) Instrument amplitude for floor motion ug0 = 0.05 mm at 15 Hz and 8 Hz; above which frequency is TR < 1? (c) WD, Ws and WD/(4πWs) at β = 1 and 0.5.由力試驗求隔振墊性質；地板振動傳到儀器的振幅；每圈能量與阻尼比定義。
Solution · 解題步驟與答案
  1. Peak 0.622 mm at 7.97 Hz. Resonance amplification: \(k = m(2\pi f_n)^2\), \(\rho_s = p_0/k\), \(U_{max}/\rho_s = 1/(2\xi\sqrt{1-\xi^2})\), corrected with \(f_{peak} = f_n\sqrt{1-2\xi^2}\) → ξ = 7.94 %. Half-power: f1 = 7.28 Hz, f2 = 8.56 Hz, \(\xi = (f_2-f_1)/(f_2+f_1) = 8.11\,\%\).共振放大法與半功率法兩種求法。
  2. Adopt fn = 8.02 Hz and ξ = 8.0 %: k = 5079 kN/m, \(c = 2\xi\sqrt{km} = 16.2\) kN·s/m.取兩法平均。
  3. (b) \(U = u_{g0}\,TR\), \(TR = D\sqrt{1+(2\xi\beta)^2}\). 15 Hz: β = 1.87, TR = 0.415 → 0.021 mm (reduced); 8 Hz: β ≈ 1, TR = 6.3 → 0.316 mm (resonance). TR = 1 at β = √2, so \(f > \sqrt2f_n = 11.3\) Hz is needed for isolation.β 大於 √2 才減振。

Viscous energy loss per cycle is proportional to frequency, whereas hysteretic soil damping is frequency independent, so soils define \(\xi = W_D/4\pi W_s\) without the frequency ratio.黏滯阻尼耗能與頻率成正比；土壤遲滯阻尼與頻率無關，所以定義中不含頻率比。

Answer: fn = 8.02 Hz, k = 5079 kN/m, ξ ≈ 8 %, c = 16.2 kN·s/m; 0.021 mm at 15 Hz and 0.32 mm at 8 Hz; isolation above 11.3 Hz.

點震源 P 波折射：x = 7.5、15.0、23.0、30.5、45.5、61.0、76.0、91.5 m 的初達時間 49.08、81.96、122.8、148.2、174.2、202.8、228.6、256.7 ms。γ = 17.5 kN/m³、ν = 0.3。求第一層的 E、G、厚度 z；第二層的 E、G。提示 \(z = \frac{x_c}{2}\sqrt{(V_2-V_1)/(V_2+V_1)}\)。
解題步驟與答案
  1. 走時曲線在 30–45 m 之間轉折。前 4 點迴歸：斜率 4.40 ms/m → V1 = 228 m/s（截距約 17 ms，不通過原點）；後 4 點：斜率 1.79 ms/m → V2 = 560 m/s。
  2. 兩直線交點 xc = 29.2 m；z = (29.2/2)√(332/788) = 9.5 m。
  3. ρ = 17.5/9.81 = 1.784 t/m³；由 Vp 求 M = ρVp²，\(E = M\frac{(1+\nu)(1-2\nu)}{1-\nu}\)、G = E/2(1+ν)：第一層 E = 68.6 MPa、G = 26.4 MPa；第二層 E = 415 MPa、G = 160 MPa。

答：V1 ≈ 228、V2 ≈ 560 m/s；z ≈ 9.5 m；E1 = 69、G1 = 26 MPa；E2 = 415、G2 = 160 MPa。若直達波強迫通過原點，V1 只有 195 m/s，答案會不同，作答時要說明怎麼擬合。

N.18

由 Hardin & Richart 建立 G–γ 關係

107 年 · 30 分
0–10 m 砂土，地下水位 2 m；e = 0.8、水位以上 w = 10 %、φ′ = 30°、Gs = 2.65。說明 8 m 深處如何建立完整的 G–γ 關係（提示 \(G_{max} = 3230\frac{(2.97-e)^2}{1+e}\sqrt{\sigma'_0}\)，kPa）。
解題步驟與答案
  1. 單位重：γd = 2.65(9.81)/1.8 = 14.44；水位以上 γ = 14.44(1.1) = 15.89；γsat = (2.65+0.8)(9.81)/1.8 = 18.80 kN/m³。
  2. 8 m：σv = 15.89(2) + 18.80(6) = 144.6 kPa，u = 58.9 kPa，σ′v = 85.7 kPa；K0 = 1 − sin30° = 0.5；σ′0 = σ′m = (2/3)(85.7) = 57.2 kPa。
  3. Gmax = 3230(2.17)²/1.8 × √57.2 = 63.9 MPa。
  4. 剪力強度：\(\tau_{max} = \sqrt{\left[\frac{1+K_0}{2}\sigma'_v\sin\phi'\right]^2 - \left[\frac{1-K_0}{2}\sigma'_v\right]^2} = 24.0\) kPa；γr = τmax/Gmax = 0.0375 %。
  5. 雙曲線 \(G = G_{max}/(1+\gamma/\gamma_r)\)：γ = 0.001 %、0.01 %、0.1 % 時 G/Gmax = 0.97、0.79、0.27；阻尼可再由 Masing 或經驗曲線得到。

答：σ′0 = 57 kPa、Gmax = 64 MPa、τmax = 24 kPa、γr = 0.038 %，G = Gmax/(1+γ/γr)。

N.24

Seed 簡易法的平均反覆剪應力

105 年 · (二) 10 分
地下水位 4 m；水位以上 γ = 18.0、以下 18.5 kN/m³；ah,max = 0.23g；rd = 1 − 0.015z。求 3 m 與 6 m 處的平均反覆剪應力。
解題步驟與答案
  1. \(\tau_{av} = 0.65\frac{a_{max}}{g}\sigma_v r_d\)（用總應力）。
  2. 3 m：σv = 54.0 kPa，rd = 0.955，τav = 0.65(0.23)(54.0)(0.955) = 7.71 kPa。
  3. 6 m：σv = 18.0(4) + 18.5(2) = 109.0 kPa，rd = 0.910，τav = 14.83 kPa。

答：7.7 kPa、14.8 kPa（CSR 還要除以 σ′v0；3 m 在水位以上不會液化）。

觀念：13.3
