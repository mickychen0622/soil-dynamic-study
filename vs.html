符號與單位

全單元只有一條核心關係：量到 Vs，就得到小應變剪力模數。單位用 ρ（t/m³）× Vs²（m²/s²）＝ kPa，最順手。

\[ G_{max} = \rho V_s^2,\qquad \rho = \frac{\gamma}{g} \]

名詞解釋

作答順序：一句定義 → 公式 → 一兩個關鍵性質。量測方法再加一句「優點／限制」，比較題就能直接拿來用。

#名詞說明詳細說明
1Small-strain shear modulus GmaxHW3小應變剪力模數

The shear modulus of soil at very small strains, where the response is linear elastic; also written G0.\(G_{max} = \rho V_s^2\); it is almost always obtained from the shear-wave velocity, because static tests can rarely measure such small strains."Max" means small-strain: it is the anchor of the G/Gmax curve, not the largest stiffness the soil can have.It increases with mean effective stress (≈ σ′m0.5), age and cementation, and decreases with void ratio.

土壤在極小應變（線彈性）時的剪力模數，也寫作 G0。靜態試驗很難量到那麼小的應變，所以幾乎都由剪力波速求得。「max」是小應變的意思，是模數折減曲線的起點，不是土壤可能的最大勁度。隨平均有效應力、年代與膠結增加，隨孔隙比增大而減小。

7.1 →
2Empirical Gmax correlationsGmax 經驗式

Formulas that estimate Gmax from void ratio, OCR and σ′m (Hardin 1978; Hardin & Drnevich 1972) or from SPT blow counts (Seed et al. 1986; Imai & Tonouchi 1982).Hardin: \(G_{max} = 625\,F(e)\,OCR^k\sqrt{p_a\sigma'_m}\), \(F(e) = 1/(0.3+0.7e^2)\).The scatter is a factor of 2 or more, so they are used only for preliminary design or to check measured Vs.Hardin's formula was fitted to unaged reconstituted specimens and often underestimates Gmax of old natural deposits.

由孔隙比、OCR、平均有效應力，或由 SPT-N 值估 Gmax 的經驗式。離散達 2 倍以上，只能做初步設計或檢核實測 Vs。Hardin 式以未老化的重模試體擬合，對老的自然沉積物常低估。

7.1 →
3Invasive and non-invasive seismic methods侵入式與非侵入式波速量測

Invasive methods need a borehole or a penetrometer: crosshole, downhole, uphole, seismic cone (SCPT), suspension P-S logging. High resolution and direct results, but costly and the hole disturbs the surrounding soil.Non-invasive methods work from the ground surface: seismic refraction and surface-wave methods (SASW, MASW, MAM). Cheap, fast and cover large areas, but they require inversion and the result is not unique.

侵入式要鑽孔或貫入（跨孔、下孔、上孔、SCPT、懸吊式 P-S 檢層）：解析度高、結果直接，但成本高、孔周擾動。非侵入式在地表施作（折射、SASW、MASW、MAM）：便宜、快速、涵蓋大面積，但要反算、結果不唯一。

7.3 →
4Downhole testHW3下孔試驗

Shear waves are generated at the ground surface next to a borehole (e.g. a hammer striking a plank horizontally) and recorded by receivers at different depths in the hole.On the travel time–depth plot, the inverse of the slope of each straight segment is the Vs of that layer, and a change in slope marks a layer boundary.Interval velocity between two depths: \(V_s = \Delta z/\Delta t\); high resolution but sensitive to errors in picking arrivals.If the source is offset from the hole, shallow ray paths are inclined and the times must first be corrected to vertical.

在孔口地表激發剪力波（槌子側敲木板），由孔內不同深度的受波器記錄到時。走時–深度圖上各直線段斜率的倒數就是該層 Vs，斜率轉折處是層界。相鄰兩深度的到時差得到區間速度，解析度高但對到時判讀誤差敏感。震源離孔口有距離時，淺層射線是斜的，要先換成垂直走時。

7.3 →
5Crosshole test跨孔試驗

A source in one borehole and receivers at the same depth in two other boreholes; the wave travels horizontally through one layer.Vs = distance between the two receiver holes / difference in arrival times; using the difference cancels the error in the trigger time.The hole spacing is critical, and deep holes drift, so a borehole deviation survey is required.

震源在一孔、受波器在另外兩孔的同一深度，波水平穿過同一層。Vs = 兩受波孔距離 / 到時差，相減可消去觸發時間的誤差。孔距非常關鍵，深孔會偏斜，必須做鑽孔偏斜量測。

7.3 →
6Seismic cone penetration test (SCPT)地震錐貫入試驗

A downhole test performed with a cone penetrometer that carries a geophone: the source is at the surface and the receiver advances with the cone.One push gives qc, fs and Vs without a borehole.Limitation: the cone cannot penetrate gravel layers.

裝有受波器的圓錐貫入儀做下孔試驗，一次貫入同時得到 qc、fs 與 Vs，不必鑽孔；但打不穿礫石層。

7.3 →
7Suspension P-S logging懸吊式 P-S 波速檢層

A probe about 7 m long, with one source and two receivers about 1 m apart, is lowered into a fluid-filled borehole.Vs = receiver spacing / difference in arrival times.Because the source moves with the probe, the resolution (about 1 m) does not decrease with depth; it can reach several hundred metres and suits deep profiles and rock.

約 7 m 長的探頭放入充液孔，一個震源、兩個相距約 1 m 的受波器，Vs = 受波器間距 / 到時差。震源跟著探頭走，深處解析度不降（約 1 m），可達數百公尺；國震中心強震站的 Vs30 多由此量得。

7.3 →
8Seismic refraction震測折射法

First-arrival times from a surface source are recorded along a line; the travel time–distance plot has a direct-wave branch and refracted-wave branches.The inverse slopes give the layer velocities; the crossover distance or intercept time gives the layer thickness.It requires velocity to increase with depth, so a low-velocity layer beneath a faster one is missed.

地表震源、沿測線記錄初達時間；走時–距離圖分為直達波與折射波兩段，斜率倒數為各層波速，交越距離或截距時間求層厚。要求波速隨深度增加，會漏掉低速夾層。

7.3 →
9Surface-wave methods (SASW, MASW, MAM)表面波法

They use the dispersion of Rayleigh waves in layered soil: short wavelengths (high frequencies) sense only shallow layers, long wavelengths (low frequencies) sense deeper layers.SASW uses 2 receivers; MASW uses 24 or more receivers and an active source; MAM records ambient vibration with a 2-D array, which is rich in low frequencies and sees deeper.The Vs profile is found by inversion: it is adjusted until the theoretical dispersion curve matches the field curve.

利用層狀土中 Rayleigh 波的頻散：短波長只感受淺層、長波長感受深層。SASW 用 2 個受波器，MASW 用 24 個以上加主動震源，MAM 用二維陣列記錄環境微動、低頻豐富而看得更深。反算時調整 Vs 剖面直到理論與現地頻散曲線吻合。

7.3 →7.2 →
10MASWHW2多頻道表面波分析

Multichannel Analysis of Surface Waves: a hammer source and a line of equally spaced geophones record Rayleigh waves.Three steps: acquisition → dispersion analysis (phase-shift method) → inversion for the Vs profile.Rules of thumb: λmax ≈ L, representative depth z ≈ λ/3, Vs ≈ 1.1c.Non-destructive; it measures small-strain stiffness, because Rayleigh-wave velocity is controlled by Vs and is almost insensitive to Vp and density.

錘擊震源＋一排等間距受波器記錄 Rayleigh 波：擷取 → 頻散分析（相位偏移法）→ 反算 Vs 剖面。經驗法則 λmax ≈ L、z ≈ λ/3、Vs ≈ 1.1c。非破壞，量到的是小應變勁度；Rayleigh 波速由 Vs 控制，對 Vp、密度幾乎不敏感。

7.2 →HW2 Q1
11Dispersion curve and dispersion image頻散曲線與頻散影像

Dispersion: in layered soil the surface-wave phase velocity changes with frequency; when stiffness increases with depth, low frequencies travel faster.Phase-shift method: each trace is Fourier-transformed and normalized to keep only its phase; for each frequency and trial velocity c, the traces are phase-shifted and summed. The sum is largest when c is correct.Plotting this sum over (f, c) gives the dispersion image; picking along the fundamental-mode ridge gives the experimental dispersion curve.

頻散：層狀土中表面波相速度隨頻率改變；隨深度變硬時低頻較快。相位偏移法：每條 trace 做 Fourier 轉換、只留相位，對每個頻率與試算相速度相位補償後相加，c 對了總和最大。對 (f, c) 作圖就是頻散影像，沿基本模態亮帶拾取就是實驗頻散曲線。只留相位是為了不讓近震源的大振幅 trace 主導。

7.2 →
12Higher modes (mode misidentification)HW2高階模態誤判

For a stiff crust over soft clay, the fundamental mode is weak at high frequency and the maximum of the dispersion image jumps to a faster higher mode.An inversion that treats every point as the fundamental mode then invents a false stiff layer (HW2 Q1(b): average Vs of 2–8 m overestimated by 36 %).Remedy: pick the lowest-velocity peak or pick manually along the fundamental mode, and check the picks for sudden jumps.

硬殼覆於軟黏土時，基本模態在高頻很弱，影像最大值跳到較快的高階模態；反算把每點都當基本模態，就造出假的硬層（HW2 Q1(b) 2–8 m 平均 Vs 高估 36 %）。對策：取最低速度峰或手動沿基本模態拾取，檢查拾取點有無跳躍。

7.2 →HW2 Q1
13Bender element test彎曲元件試驗

Two bonded piezoceramic plates of opposite polarity bend like a diving board when a voltage is applied; one element at each end of the specimen sends and receives a shear wave.\(V_s = L_{tt}/t\), where Ltt is the tip-to-tip distance (not the specimen height); \(G_{max} = \rho V_s^2\).It can be installed in a triaxial cell and used at any stage (consolidation, shearing, after cyclic loading), but it gives only Gmax.

兩片極性相反的壓電陶瓷黏在一起，加電壓時像跳水板彎曲；試體兩端各一片，一發一收剪力波。Vs = 尖端間距 / 走時（不是試體高度），Gmax = ρVs²。可裝在三軸室內隨時量，但應變極小，只得到 Gmax。

7.4 →
14Near-field effect近場效應

When the source is small and close to the receiver, a small reversed disturbance arrives before the shear wave, travelling at about the P-wave velocity.Taking it as the arrival makes t too short and Vs too high.Remedy: raise the excitation frequency so that the path contains enough wavelengths (L/λ > 2), and confirm that the picked arrival does not change with frequency.

波源小又近時，剪力波之前會先到一個小的反向擾動（以接近 P 波的速度傳來）。誤當成抵達 → t 偏小、Vs 偏高。對策：提高激振頻率使 L/λ > 2，再用幾個頻率確認拾取的到時不變。

7.4 →
15Field versus laboratory GmaxHW3現地與室內 Gmax

In theory MASW, resonant column and bender element tests give the same Gmax; in practice they often differ by 20–50 %.Reasons: sampling disturbance (stress release, broken particle contacts, lost aging and cementation), stress state (isotropic in the cell, anisotropic in the field) and scale (a 10 cm specimen versus a 30 m layer).Practice: take Gmax from the field and the shape of the G/Gmax and damping curves from the laboratory.

理論上相同，實務常差 20–50 %：取樣擾動（應力釋放、顆粒接觸破壞、老化與膠結消失）、應力狀態（室內等向、現地非等向）、尺度（10 cm 試體與 30 m 土層）。實務上 Gmax 取自現地，曲線形狀取自實驗室。HW3 Q3 第 3 層：現地 140.9 MPa，Hardin 97.1、共振柱 93.2 MPa。

7.4 →HW3 Q3
16Vs30HW2地表下 30 m 平均剪力波速

The time-averaged shear-wave velocity of the top 30 m: \(V_{s30} = 30/\sum(h_i/V_{si})\).The denominator is the vertical travel time through 30 m, so Vs30 is the uniform velocity that takes the same time, not the thickness-weighted arithmetic average.Soft layers dominate it, just as they dominate site period and impedance contrast, which is why codes use it for site classification.

\(V_{s30} = 30/\sum(h_i/V_{si})\)：分母是剪力波垂直穿過 30 m 的總走時，所以是走時平均，不是厚度加權的算術平均。軟層主導走時，也主導場址週期與阻抗對比，所以規範用它分類。

7.5 →HW2 Q2
17Site classificationHW2場址（地盤）分類

Seismic codes group sites by Vs30 to select site amplification.NEHRP / ASCE 7: A (> 1500), B (760–1500), C (360–760), D (180–360), E (< 180), F (special).Eurocode 8: A (> 800), B (360–800), C (180–360), D (< 180), S1 and S2 (special).Taiwan code: Class 1 (firm) Vs30 ≥ 270; Class 2 (normal) 180–270; Class 3 (soft) < 180; the Taipei Basin uses microzones instead.

規範依 Vs30 分類以決定放大。NEHRP 分 A–E 加特殊類 F；Eurocode 8 分 A–D 加 S1、S2；臺灣規範（111 年版）第一類 ≥ 270、第二類 180–270、第三類 < 180 m/s，臺北盆地依臺北一、二、三區微分區，不用 Vs30。引用類別一定要註明規範。

18Site coefficients Fa and Fv工址放大係數

Factors from Table 2-4(a), (b) of the Taiwan code that scale the short-period (Fa) and long-period (Fv) design spectral accelerations by site class.Softer sites amplify more; at stronger shaking levels the amplification is smaller because the soil becomes nonlinear.

依地盤分類查耐震規範表 2-4(a)、(b)，放大短週期（Fa）與長週期（Fv）設計譜加速度。地盤越軟放大越多；震區越強放大越少（土壤非線性）。

簡答題的關鍵觀念

名詞解釋之外，常被問「為什麼」或「比較」。每題先寫結論，再寫一句理由。

#問題答題重點詳細說明
1Why is Gmax measured with waves?為什麼 Gmax 都用波速量？

Wave tests (field and bender element) impose very small strains, exactly the linear elastic range that defines Gmax; static tests rarely resolve such small strains.Vs reflects only the soil skeleton, so Gmax = ρVs² follows directly.

波動試驗的應變極小，正好是定義 Gmax 的線彈性範圍；靜態試驗很少量得到。Vs 只反映土骨架，直接 Gmax = ρVs²。

7.1 →
2What factors affect Gmax?影響 Gmax 的因素

Mean effective stress: Gmax ∝ σ′m0.5 (the most important).Void ratio: looser soil, lower Gmax.Age and cementation: increase it, and are destroyed by sampling.OCR: mainly for clays, with an effect that grows with PI.Saturation: small effect for sands; water only adds mass.

平均有效應力（最重要）、孔隙比、年代與膠結（取樣會破壞）、OCR（黏土，隨 PI 增大）、飽和度（對砂影響小）。

7.1 →
3Compare invasive and surface-wave methods鑽孔法與表面波法比較

Borehole methods (downhole, crosshole, SCPT, suspension): direct, high resolution, but expensive, local, and the hole disturbs the soil.Surface-wave methods (MASW, SASW, MAM): cheap, fast, non-destructive, average over the array, but need inversion with non-unique results and struggle with stiff-over-soft layering.Best practice: combine them; one borehole constrains the inversion.

鑽孔法直接、解析度高，但貴、只代表一點、孔周擾動；表面波法便宜、快、非破壞、給測線下方平均剖面，但要反算、結果不唯一，硬層在軟層上時困難。一個鑽孔是約束反算最好的方法。

7.3 →
4Why does MASW need low frequencies and a long array to see deep?為什麼看深層要低頻、長測線？

A Rayleigh wave's motion decays within about one wavelength, so only long wavelengths (low frequencies, λ = c/f) are affected by deep layers.The array length limits λmax ≈ L, and the source must produce low-frequency energy (a heavier hammer); both are needed.

Rayleigh 波振幅約在一個波長內衰減，只有長波長（低頻）會受深層影響。測線長度限制 λmax，震源的低頻能量也限制最低可用頻率，兩者都要夠。

7.2 →
5Why does MASW measure Vs and not Vp?HW2為什麼 MASW 量的是 Vs？

Rayleigh-wave velocity is controlled by shear stiffness (cR/Vs only changes from about 0.92 to 0.95 between ν = 0.25 and 0.49).In HW2 Q1(c), a fourfold increase in Vp (saturation) left the dispersion curve almost unchanged, so the data cannot resolve ν.

Rayleigh 波由剪力主導，對 Vp、密度幾乎不敏感；HW2 Q1(c) 飽和使 Vp 增加約四倍，頻散曲線幾乎不變。

7.2 →HW2 Q1 →
6What goes wrong with higher modes?HW2高階模態會造成什麼錯？

Higher modes are always faster than the fundamental mode; if their points are inverted as the fundamental mode, a false shallow stiff layer appears.Most likely when a stiff crust overlies soft clay (velocity reversal).Check the image, pick along the fundamental mode, and look for jumps in the picked curve.

高階模態永遠比較快；當成基本模態反算會造出淺層假硬層，常見於硬殼覆軟黏土（速度反轉）。要看頻散影像、沿基本模態拾取、檢查跳躍。

7.2 →
7Why does the crosshole test use two receiver holes?跨孔為什麼要兩個受波孔？

Using the arrival-time difference between two receivers cancels the uncertainty of the trigger time at the source.Because Vs = Δd/Δt, an error in hole spacing goes directly into Vs; hence the deviation survey.

兩受波器相減消去觸發時間誤差；孔距誤差直接進到 Vs，所以要做偏斜量測。

7.3 →
8Where is the arrival in a bender element signal?彎曲元件的到時怎麼判？

Not at the first small reversed deflection: that is the near-field component, and picking it overestimates Vs.Use a frequency high enough for L/λ > 2 and check that the pick is the same for several input frequencies; measure L tip to tip.

第一個小的反向擾動是近場成分，不是剪力波到達；提高頻率使 L/λ > 2，用不同頻率確認到時不變，L 用尖端間距。

7.4 →
9Why do field and laboratory Gmax differ, and which is used?HW3現地與室內 Gmax 為何不同？用哪個？

Laboratory values are usually lower because of sampling disturbance (lost fabric, aging, cementation), isotropic versus anisotropic stress, and scale.Use field Gmax for the level and laboratory curves for the shape: G = (G/Gmax)lab × ρVs,field².

室內值常偏低（取樣擾動、應力狀態、尺度）。大小取現地 Gmax，形狀取室內曲線。HW3 Q3：現地 140.9、Hardin 97.1、共振柱 93.2 MPa；模擬考 6.4：148.7、107.0、104.4 MPa。

7.4 →HW3 Q3 →
10Why is Vs30 a travel-time average?HW2為什麼 Vs30 用走時平均？

Site response is governed by the travel time through the deposit (site period T ≈ 4H/Vs) and by the impedance contrast, and both are dominated by the soft layers.The arithmetic average weights by thickness and hides thin soft layers: in HW2 Q2(c) a 10 m clay layer is one third of the thickness but 79 % of the travel time (189.5 versus 426.7 m/s).

場址反應由走時（週期）與阻抗對比控制，兩者都由軟層主導；算術平均以厚度加權，會把薄軟層藏起來而高估一整類，而這正是放大最強的場址。

7.5 →HW2 Q2 →
11Limitations of Vs30 classificationVs30 分類的限制

It cannot describe liquefiable soils, very thick soft clay or sensitive clay, so every code has special categories (NEHRP F, EC8 S1/S2, Taipei Basin microzones).The same Vs30 has different class names in different codes (300 m/s: NEHRP D, EC8 C, Taiwan Class 1).

可液化土、極厚軟黏土、敏感黏土描述不了，所以各規範都有特殊類別；同一數值在不同規範類別名稱不同。

常見陷阱與計算機提醒

  • Vs30 用走時平均：算術平均會高估一整類；HW2 Q2 的 303 m/s 在臺灣會誤判成第一類（正確 251.7 m/s，第二類）。
  • 在 30 m 截斷：最後一層只算 30 m 以上的部分（N.7 只取 3 m 的 420 m/s 層；N.13 只取 2 m 的 400 m/s 層）。
  • 分類要註明規範：同一個 300 m/s，NEHRP D、EC8 C、臺灣第一類。臺灣 270 本身屬第一類；岩盤界線 NEHRP 760、EC8 800。
  • 單位：ρ（t/m³）× Vs²（m/s）= kPa；給 γ（kN/m³）時要除以 g；斜率 ms/m 時 Vs = 1000/斜率。
  • 經驗式的單位：Seed 式用 psf、Imai 式用 ksf（1 psf = 47.88 Pa）；Hardin & Drnevich 式的 σ′m 用 kPa；Hardin 1978 同單位即可。
  • 彎曲元件的 L：是尖端間距 H − 2 × 插入深度；用試體高度會高估 G（L 差 6 %、G 差 13 %）。
  • 近場效應：把剪力波前的小擾動當到時 → t 偏小、Vs 偏高。
  • 下孔迴歸：第一層要含原點；層界看斜率轉折；震源有偏距時淺層先做斜射修正。
  • MASW 的 λ/3 是起點不是答案：硬殼、軟夾層、速度反轉時會錯很多；Vs ≈ 1.1c 只對基本模態；λ > L 的點不可信。
  • Gmax 的「max」：意思是小應變，不是最大可能勁度；也寫作 G0。
  • 符號撞名：本單元的 c 是 Rayleigh 波相速度（單元 4 是阻尼係數）；γ 是單位重（模數折減曲線等處的 γ 是剪應變）。
