符號與運動方程式

SDOF 只用一個位移 \(u\) 描述運動。三個系統參數是 m 質量、c 阻尼係數、k 勁度；\(u\) 是位移，不是參數。

\[ m\ddot u + c\dot u + ku = p(t) \qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad \ddot u + 2\xi\omega_n\dot u + \omega_n^2u = \frac{p(t)}{m} \]
符號意義常用單位
\(m\)Mass 質量。題目給重量時 \(m = W/g\)t（= kN·s²/m）
\(c\)Damping (dashpot) coefficient 阻尼係數kN·s/m
\(k\)Stiffness 勁度 = 力 / 靜位移kN/m
\(u,\ \dot u,\ \ddot u\)Displacement, velocity, acceleration 位移、速度、加速度m、m/s、m/s²
\(u_0,\ \dot u_0\)Initial displacement and velocity 初始位移、初速m、m/s
\(p(t) = p_0\sin\bar\omega t\)Harmonic load 簡諧外力；\(p_0\) 幅值、\(\bar\omega\) 外力頻率kN、rad/s
\(\omega_n,\ f_n,\ T_n\)Natural circular frequency, frequency, period 自然圓頻率、頻率、週期rad/s、Hz、s
\(\omega_D\)Damped natural frequency 阻尼自然頻率rad/s
\(c_c\)Critical damping coefficient 臨界阻尼係數kN·s/m
\(\xi\)Damping ratio 阻尼比（單元 8、9 寫作 D）—
\(\beta = \bar\omega/\omega_n\)Frequency ratio 頻率比—
\(\rho_s = p_0/k\)Static displacement 靜態變位m
\(D\)Dynamic magnification factor 動態放大因子—
\(\rho_d = \rho_sD\)Steady-state amplitude 穩態振幅m

\(\delta\)Logarithmic decrement 對數遞減—

名詞解釋

作答順序：一句定義 → 公式 → 一兩個關鍵性質。2025 年的格式是「briefly describe in English/Chinese what you know about…」，每題 4 分。

#名詞說明詳細推導
1Single-degree-of-freedom (SDOF) system單自由度系統

A system whose motion is fully described by one displacement coordinate u.It consists of a mass m (inertia), a spring k (stiffness, stores strain energy) and a dashpot c (viscous damping, dissipates energy).Equation of motion: \(m\ddot u + c\dot u + ku = p(t)\).A machine foundation, a resonant-column specimen, a soil layer and a base-isolated building can all be idealized as SDOF systems.

只需一個位移座標 u 就能描述運動的系統，由質量 m（慣性）、彈簧 k（勁度，儲存應變能）、阻尼器 c（黏滯阻尼，消散能量）組成。機器基礎、共振柱土樣、土層、隔震建築都可簡化成 SDOF。

4.1 →
2Natural frequency自然頻率

The frequency at which a system vibrates freely after being disturbed, without damping or external force.\(\omega_n = \sqrt{k/m}\) (rad/s), \(f_n = \omega_n/2\pi\) (Hz), \(T_n = 1/f_n = 2\pi/\omega_n\) (s).It depends only on m and k, not on the initial conditions or the amplitude.

系統受擾動後、無阻尼也無外力時自由振動的頻率。只由 m、k 決定，與初始條件、振幅無關；剛度越大、質量越小，頻率越高。

4.2 →
3Damped natural frequency阻尼自然頻率

The frequency of damped free vibration: \(\omega_D = \omega_n\sqrt{1-\xi^2}\), \(T_D = 2\pi/\omega_D\).It is slightly lower than ωn; for ξ ≤ 0.2 the difference is at most 2 %, so ωD ≈ ωn in practice.

有阻尼自由振動的頻率，略低於 ωn；ξ ≤ 0.2 時只差 2 % 以內，工程上可取 ωD ≈ ωn。

4.3 →
4Critical damping coefficient★臨界阻尼係數

The smallest damping coefficient for which a displaced system returns to equilibrium without oscillating.\(c_c = 2m\omega_n = 2\sqrt{km}\), obtained when the characteristic equation \(m\lambda^2 + c\lambda + k = 0\) has a double root.A critically damped system returns to equilibrium fastest without overshoot.It is the reference value for the damping ratio, ξ = c/cc.

讓位移後的系統不振盪就回到平衡的最小阻尼係數，\(c_c = 2m\omega_n = 2\sqrt{km}\)（特徵方程式出現重根）。臨界阻尼是「不振盪」中回得最快的；它是阻尼比的基準，ξ = c/cc。

4.3 →
5Damping ratio ξ阻尼比

The ratio of the actual damping coefficient to the critical damping coefficient: \(\xi = c/c_c = c/(2m\omega_n)\).Energy definition: \(\xi = W_D/(4\pi W_S)\), where WD is the energy dissipated per cycle and WS the maximum strain energy.Typical values: structures 2–5 %; soils 1–3 % at small strain and 15–25 % at large strain; footings on soil 20–40 % (radiation damping).

實際阻尼係數 / 臨界阻尼係數，無因次。能量定義 ξ = WD/(4πWS)，可推廣到土壤的遲滯迴圈。結構約 2–5 %，土壤小應變 1–3 %、大應變 15–25 %，地表基礎因輻射阻尼可達 20–40 %。

4.3 →4.8 →
6Underdamped, critically damped and overdamped次阻尼、臨界阻尼、超阻尼

ξ < 1 (underdamped): oscillates with an exponentially decaying amplitude, \(u = e^{-\xi\omega_nt}(A\cos\omega_Dt + B\sin\omega_Dt)\).ξ = 1 (critically damped): no oscillation; the fastest return to equilibrium.ξ > 1 (overdamped): no oscillation; creeps back more slowly than the critical case.Soils and structures are always underdamped.

ξ < 1 邊振動邊衰減；ξ = 1 不振動、最快回到平衡；ξ > 1 不振動、慢慢爬回（比臨界還慢）。土壤與結構都屬次阻尼。

4.3 →
7Logarithmic decrement δ對數遞減

The natural logarithm of the ratio of two successive peak amplitudes in damped free vibration.\(\delta = \ln(u_1/u_2) = 2\pi\xi/\sqrt{1-\xi^2} \approx 2\pi\xi\); over n cycles, \(\delta = \frac1n\ln(u_1/u_{n+1})\).It is used to measure damping from a free-decay record, e.g. in the resonant column test.

有阻尼自由振動中相鄰兩峰值比的自然對數。相鄰兩峰差很小，實務上隔 n 圈量較準。共振柱關掉驅動後的自由衰減就是用它求阻尼比。

4.9 →
8Transient and steady-state response暫態與穩態反應

Under harmonic loading, total response = transient response + steady-state response.Transient: the homogeneous solution; it vibrates at ωD, decays as \(e^{-\xi\omega_nt}\) and depends on the initial conditions.Steady state: the particular solution; it vibrates at the forcing frequency ω̄ with constant amplitude ρsD and phase lag φ, independent of the initial conditions.

受簡諧外力時，總反應 = 暫態（齊次解，以 ωD 振動、逐漸衰減，由初始條件決定）＋穩態（特解，以外力頻率振動，振幅 ρsD、相位落後 φ，與初始條件無關）。

4.4 →
9Resonance★共振

The condition in which the forcing frequency equals the natural frequency (β = ω̄/ωn = 1).The spring force and the inertia force cancel each other, so the load is resisted by the damping force alone.The steady-state amplitude becomes ρs/(2ξ) (D = 1/2ξ), and the displacement lags the load by 90°.Without damping the amplitude grows without limit (by πρs per cycle); with damping it builds up over about 1/(2πξ) cycles to reach 63 % of the steady value.

外力頻率等於自然頻率（β = 1）。彈簧力與慣性力抵消，外力只由阻尼力平衡，振幅放大為 1/(2ξ) 倍、位移落後外力 90°。無阻尼時振幅隨時間線性成長（每圈增加 πρs）；有阻尼時要約 1/(2πξ) 圈才累積到穩態的 63 %。

4.5 →4.7 →
10Dynamic magnification factor D★動態放大因子

The ratio of the steady-state dynamic amplitude to the static displacement ρs = p0/k.\(D = 1/\sqrt{(1-\beta^2)^2 + (2\xi\beta)^2}\); it depends only on β and ξ.β ≪ 1: D ≈ 1 (stiffness-controlled); β = 1: D = 1/(2ξ) (damping-controlled); β ≫ 1: D ≈ 1/β² → 0 (inertia-controlled).Peak \(D_{max} = 1/(2\xi\sqrt{1-\xi^2})\) at \(\beta = \sqrt{1-2\xi^2}\); no peak when ξ ≥ 0.707.

穩態動態振幅 / 靜態變位，只由 β、ξ 決定。β ≪ 1 勁度控制（D ≈ 1）、β = 1 阻尼控制（D = 1/2ξ）、β ≫ 1 慣性控制（D → 0）。峰值略低於 β = 1；ξ ≥ 0.707 沒有峰。

4.4 →4.5 →
11Phase angle φ相位角（相位落後）

The angle by which the steady-state displacement lags behind the applied force.\(\tan\varphi = 2\xi\beta/(1-\beta^2)\): φ ≈ 0° for β ≪ 1, φ = 90° at β = 1 for any ξ, φ → 180° for β ≫ 1.The 90° lag is used to identify resonance (e.g. resonant column test) without knowing the damping.

穩態位移落後外力的角度。低頻同相、共振時 90°（與阻尼無關）、高頻接近反相 180°。共振柱用「落後 90°」來找共振頻率。

4.4 →4.5 →
12Half-power bandwidth methodHW3半功率頻寬法

A method to estimate damping from a frequency-response curve.Find f1 and f2 where the amplitude equals peak/√2 (the energy is half of the peak value): \(\xi \approx (f_2-f_1)/(2f_n) \approx (f_2-f_1)/(f_2+f_1)\).It needs only the shape of the curve, not the force magnitude or the static displacement.It overestimates ξ when the response is nonlinear (large strain).

在頻率響應曲線上找振幅等於峰值 1/√2 的兩個頻率（能量為峰值的一半）。只需曲線形狀、不需外力大小；大應變時曲線傾斜會高估阻尼。

4.9 →
13Resonance amplification methodHW3共振放大法

At resonance D ≈ 1/(2ξ), so \(\xi \approx \rho_s/(2U_{max})\), with ρs = p0/k.It requires the static displacement, which is often too small to measure.If coarse frequency steps miss the true peak, Umax is too small and ξ is overestimated.

共振振幅 / 靜態變位 ≈ 1/(2ξ)。需要靜態變位（常常太小量不到）；掃頻太粗錯過峰頂，會高估阻尼。

4.9 →
14Transmissibility and vibration isolationHW3傳遞率與隔振

TR1 = force transmitted to the support / applied force (isolating a vibrating machine).TR2 = amplitude of the mass / amplitude of the base motion (isolating a sensitive instrument).\(TR_1 = TR_2 = D\sqrt{1+(2\xi\beta)^2}\).Isolation (TR < 1) requires β > √2, i.e. a soft support with fn < f̄/√2; in this range more damping increases TR.

TR1：機器的力傳到地面的比例；TR2：地面振動傳到質量的比例，兩者是同一個函數。β > √2 才有隔振效果，所以支承要軟；在隔振區阻尼越大傳越多。

4.10 →
15Energy dissipated per cycleHW3每圈消散能量

\(W_D = \oint c\dot u\,du = \pi c\bar\omega\rho_d^2\): the area of the force–displacement loop.Maximum strain energy \(W_S = \tfrac12k\rho_d^2\); for a viscous damper \(W_D/(4\pi W_S) = \xi\beta\), which equals ξ only at resonance.Soil damping is hysteretic (nearly frequency-independent), so for soils the damping ratio is defined directly as \(\xi = W_D/(4\pi W_S)\) from one hysteresis loop.

阻尼力一圈做的功＝力–位移迴圈面積。黏滯阻尼的比值 = ξβ，只有共振時等於 ξ；土壤每圈消散的能量幾乎與頻率無關（摩擦遲滯），所以直接用一個迴圈定義 ξ = WD/(4πWS)。

4.8 →
16Lysmer's analogLysmer 類比

Represents a rigid circular footing (radius r0) vibrating vertically on an elastic half-space (G, ν, ρ) by a frequency-independent spring and dashpot.\(k_z = 4Gr_0/(1-\nu)\), \(c_z = 3.4r_0^2\sqrt{G\rho}/(1-\nu)\); then \(m\ddot z + c_z\dot z + k_zz = Q(t)\) and all SDOF formulas apply.A rectangular footing uses the equivalent radius \(r_0 = \sqrt{BL/\pi}\).

把半無限彈性土體上剛性圓形基礎的垂直振動，換成與頻率無關的彈簧與阻尼器，之後全部套 SDOF 公式。矩形基礎用等效半徑。

4.11 →
17Geometrical (radiation) damping and mass ratio Bz幾何（輻射）阻尼與修正質量比

Energy carried away from a vibrating foundation by waves spreading into the half-space; it never returns, so it acts like a dashpot without any physical damper.For vertical vibration about 67 % of the energy goes into Rayleigh waves, 26 % into S waves and 7 % into P waves.\(B_z = (1-\nu)m/(4\rho r_0^3)\), \(\xi_z = 0.425/\sqrt{B_z}\): a light, wide footing (small Bz) has large damping.It is much reduced if rigid bedrock is shallow, because waves reflect back.

基礎振動時波往土中傳開、一去不回，帶走的能量等於阻尼。垂直振動約 2/3 能量由 Rayleigh 波帶走。Bz 小（輕而寬）→ 阻尼大；淺處有岩盤時波會反射回來，輻射阻尼大減。

4.11 →
18Rotating-mass excitation旋轉質量激振

An unbalanced mass me at eccentricity e produces \(Q_0 = m_ee\,\bar\omega^2\), which grows with the square of the speed.Amplitude \(A = (m_ee/m)\,\beta^2D\); peak at \(\beta = 1/\sqrt{1-2\xi^2}\) (above fn); at high speed A → mee/m.For a constant-amplitude force, the peak is at \(\beta = \sqrt{1-2\xi^2}\) (below fn).

偏心質量的不平衡力隨轉速平方增加，所以峰值在 fn 之上；定力激振的峰在 fn 之下。機器基礎 ξ 常達 0.3–0.4，兩者差 15–20 %，不能直接取峰在 β = 1。

B　自由振動（給初始條件）

#名稱公式與說明推導／練習
B1Undamped free vibration★無阻尼自由振動
\[ \begin{aligned} &u(t) = u_0\cos\omega_nt + \frac{\dot u_0}{\omega_n}\sin\omega_nt \\ &\rho = \sqrt{u_0^2 + \left(\frac{\dot u_0}{\omega_n}\right)^2} \end{aligned} \]

ρ 是位移振幅；最大速度 ωnρ、最大加速度 ωn²ρ。

B2Phase angle of free vibration★自由振動的相位角
\[ \begin{aligned} &u = \rho\sin(\omega_nt + \theta),\quad \tan\theta = \frac{u_0\,\omega_n}{\dot u_0} \\ &u = \rho\cos(\omega_nt - \theta'),\quad \tan\theta' = \frac{\dot u_0}{u_0\,\omega_n},\qquad \theta + \theta' = 90^\circ \end{aligned} \]

D　由試驗資料求阻尼比

簡答題的關鍵觀念

名詞解釋之外，常被問「為什麼」。每題先寫結論，再寫一句理由。

#問題答題重點詳細推導
1What controls the response in each frequency range?三個頻率區由誰控制？

β ≪ 1: stiffness-controlled; the mass follows the load, D ≈ 1, φ ≈ 0° (quasi-static).β ≈ 1: damping-controlled; D = 1/(2ξ), φ = 90°.β ≫ 1: inertia-controlled; the mass cannot follow, D → 0, φ → 180°.

低頻靠彈簧、共振靠阻尼器、高頻靠質量慣性。

4.4 →
2Why does only damping limit the resonant amplitude?為什麼共振振幅只由阻尼決定？

At β = 1 the inertia force (mω̄²u) and the spring force (ku) are equal and opposite.The load is balanced by the damping force alone: p0 = cω̄ρd, so ρd = ρs/(2ξ).To reduce resonant vibration, increase c, or move fn away from the operating frequency by changing m or k.

共振時慣性力與彈簧力抵消，只剩阻尼器扛外力。要降低共振振幅就加阻尼，或改 m、k 讓 fn 避開運轉頻率。

4.6 →
3Is the peak exactly at β = 1?峰值在 β = 1 嗎？

No. For a constant force the peak is at √(1−2ξ²), slightly below 1; for rotating-mass excitation it is at 1/√(1−2ξ²), slightly above 1.The 90° phase crossing is always at β = 1.The difference matters only for large damping (machine foundations, ξ ≈ 0.3–0.4).

定力在 β = 1 之下、旋轉質量在 β = 1 之上；90° 相位一定在 β = 1。阻尼小時差異可忽略。

4.5 →4.11 →
4Why is overdamping slower than critical damping?為什麼超阻尼反而比較慢？

The slower root, λ = −ωn(ξ − √(ξ²−1)), controls the return; for ξ = 2 it decays as e−0.27ωnt, versus e−ωnt for ξ = 1.The damper is so strong that the mass creeps back.

衰減較慢的那個根決定回復速度；阻尼太大，質量像被黏住慢慢爬回。

4.3 →
5How do you isolate vibration?隔振要怎麼設計？

Make the support soft so that β = f̄/fn > √2 (with light damping, β ≈ 3.3 gives TR ≈ 10 %).In the isolation range, more damping increases TR, because the dashpot is a second path for force.Some damping is still needed because the machine passes through resonance during start-up and shut-down.Active isolation: isolate the source (TR1); passive isolation: isolate the receiver (TR2).

支承做軟，讓 β > √2；隔振區阻尼越大傳越多，但開關機會通過共振，仍要適量阻尼。主動隔振隔震源、被動隔振保護接收者。

4.10 →
6Does a short earthquake reach the full resonant amplitude?短促的地震來得及共振嗎？

No. Resonant response builds up as (1 − e−ξωnt); reaching 63 % takes 1/(2πξ) cycles (about 3.2 cycles for ξ = 5 %).So duration matters as much as frequency content.

共振要時間累積；ξ = 5 % 約 3.2 圈才到 63 %。延時和頻率內容一樣重要。

4.7 →
7Why is soil damping defined as WD/(4πWS) without β?HW3為什麼土壤阻尼比不帶 β？

For a viscous damper WD ∝ ω̄, so WD/(4πWS) = ξβ changes with frequency.Soil damping comes from friction between particles (hysteretic damping); the loop area depends on strain amplitude, not on frequency.So ξ is defined as the viscous damping that dissipates the same energy per cycle at resonance; it is equivalent to the complex modulus G* = G(1 + 2iξ).

黏滯阻尼每圈耗能隨頻率增加；土壤是顆粒摩擦的遲滯阻尼，迴圈面積只看應變振幅。所以直接用一個迴圈定義 ξ（等於「共振時耗能相同的等值黏滯阻尼」）。

4.8 →
8Where does a footing get damping without a damper?基礎沒有阻尼器，阻尼從哪來？

Radiation (geometrical) damping: waves carry energy away into the half-space and do not return.It is controlled by the mass ratio Bz, not by soil stiffness: ξz = 0.425/√Bz.Vertical and horizontal modes have large radiation damping; rocking and torsion have small damping.

波帶走能量；ξz 只看 Bz（G 消掉）。垂直、水平模態阻尼大，搖擺、扭轉阻尼小。

4.11 →
9Machine foundation design: avoiding resonance機器基礎如何避開共振？

Keep the operating frequency well away from fn: either a high-tuned foundation (fn above the operating frequency) or a low-tuned one (fn below it).If it fails: change kz (larger footing area, ground improvement, piles) or m (heavier block), change the machine speed, or add isolation mounts.Also check that the amplitude at the operating frequency is within the allowable limit.

讓運轉頻率遠離 fn（高調或低調設計）；不行就改勁度（加大面積、地盤改良、樁）或質量、改轉速、加隔振支承；並檢核運轉振幅。

常見陷阱與計算機提醒

  • 重量不是質量：m = W/g；W 用 kN 時 m 的單位是 t，k 用 kN/m、c 用 kN·s/m，單位自然一致。
  • ω 與 f 差 2π：ω（rad/s）= 2πf（Hz）；rpm → rad/s 要乘 2π/60；T = 1/f = 2π/ω。
  • β > 1 的相位：tan⁻¹ 給負角，要加 180°，或用 atan2(2ξβ, 1 − β²)。
  • 自由振動相位角有兩種寫法：sin 形式與 cos 形式的角度互餘（期中 Q3：89.74° 與 0.26°），作答先寫出 u(t) 的形式。
  • δ 的近似：δ = 2πξ/√(1 − ξ²) 才是精確式；ξ ≈ 0.26 時 δ ≈ 2πξ 會差約 3 %，題目給 ξ 時用精確式。
  • 隔 n 圈：第 1 峰到第 n+1 峰才是 n 圈。
  • 共振峰位置：嚴格在 √(1 − 2ξ²)，不在 β = 1；機器基礎阻尼大時一定要用精確式。
  • 隔振要軟：β > √2 才減振；隔振區阻尼越大，傳遞越多。
  • 總反應的常數：用總反應（暫態＋穩態）的初始條件求，不是只代暫態。
  • D 的兩種意思：單元 4 的 D 是放大因子；單元 8、9 與國考題常把阻尼比寫成 D。
