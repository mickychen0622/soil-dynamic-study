Ch1

地震概論

#名詞說明詳細推導
1Hypocenter (focus) and epicenter震源與震央

The hypocenter (focus) is the point inside the earth where fault rupture starts.The epicenter is the point on the ground surface directly above it.Their distance is the focal depth.For large ruptures, the closest distance to the rupture plane, Rrup, is used instead.

震源是斷層開始破裂的地下點；震央是震源正上方的地表點，兩者的距離就是震源深度。大地震的破裂面很長，工程上常改用到破裂面的最短距離 Rrup。

2.3 →
2Strike and dip走向與傾角

Strike: the azimuth of the line where the fault plane intersects a horizontal surface.Dip: the angle between the fault plane and the horizontal, measured perpendicular to strike.Together with rake, they define the fault type.

走向是斷層面與水平面交線的方位；傾角是斷層面與水平面的夾角，在垂直走向的剖面上量。再加上滑移角，就能判斷斷層類型。

2.3 →
3Hanging wall and footwall上盤與下盤

For an inclined fault, the block above the fault plane is the hanging wall.The block below it is the footwall.

傾斜斷層面上方的岩塊是上盤、下方是下盤（站在斷層面上，頭頂那塊是上盤）。垂直的斷層面沒有上下盤之分。

2.3 →
4Reverse fault★逆斷層

Motion: the hanging wall is pushed up over the footwall.Cause: horizontal squeezing (σ1 horizontal, σ3 vertical), so the crust gets shorter.Thrust fault: a reverse fault whose plane dips less than about 45°, e.g. the Chelungpu fault (1999 Chi-Chi earthquake).

動向：上盤被推上去、爬到下盤上方。成因：兩側水平往中間擠（σ1 水平、σ3 垂直），地殼變短。斷層面傾角小於約 45° 的叫逆衝斷層（thrust fault），例如 1999 年集集地震的車籠埔斷層。

2.3 →
5Normal fault正斷層

Motion: the hanging wall slides down the fault plane relative to the footwall.Cause: horizontal stretching (σ1 vertical, σ3 horizontal), so the crust gets longer.

動向：上盤沿斷層面往下滑、落到比下盤低。成因：兩側水平往外拉（σ1 垂直、σ3 水平），地殼變長。

2.3 →
6Strike-slip fault平移斷層

Motion: the two blocks slide horizontally past each other along the strike.Cause: σ1 and σ3 both lie in the horizontal plane.Sense: if the block across the fault moves to the right it is right-lateral, to the left it is left-lateral; the answer is the same from either side.

動向：兩側沿走向水平錯開。成因：σ1、σ3 都在水平面上。判別：站在一側看，對面那塊往右移是右移斷層、往左移是左移斷層，站哪一側看都一樣。

2.3 →
7Elastic rebound theory彈性回跳理論

Reid (1910): relative plate motion builds up elastic strain in the rock on both sides of a locked fault.When the stress exceeds the fault strength, the fault slips suddenly.The rock rebounds to an unstrained state, and the stored strain energy is released as seismic waves.

Reid（1910）：板塊持續相對運動，斷層被摩擦鎖住，兩側岩體累積彈性應變；剪應力超過強度時突然滑動，岩體彈回，應變能以地震波釋放，然後重新開始累積。

2.4 →
8Active fault活動斷層

A fault with evidence of movement in the recent geologic past (typically the last 100,000 years) that is likely to rupture again.Taiwan defines Type I (active within 10,000 years) and Type II (within 100,000 years).

未來可能再發生地震的斷層，一般以近 10 萬年內有活動證據者認定。臺灣分第一類（近 1 萬年內活動）與第二類（近 10 萬年內），並依地質法劃定活動斷層地質敏感區。

2.4 →
9Seismic moment M0 and moment magnitude Mw地震矩與地震矩規模

Seismic moment \(M_0 = \mu AD\) (rigidity × rupture area × average slip) measures the physical size of the rupture.Moment magnitude \(M_w = (\log M_0 - 16.05)/1.5\), with M0 in dyne·cm.One unit of Mw is about 32 times in M0.Mw does not saturate.

\(M_0 = \mu AD\)（剛性模數 × 破裂面積 × 平均滑移量），直接反映破裂的大小；\(M_w = (\log M_0 - 16.05)/1.5\)（M0 以 dyne·cm 計）。ΔMw = 1 時 M0 差 32 倍；Mw 不會飽和。

2.6 →
10Magnitude saturation規模飽和

Magnitudes based on wave amplitudes at a fixed period (ML, mb, Ms) stop increasing for large earthquakes.The reason: a larger rupture adds mainly long-period energy.mb saturates near 6–6.5, ML near 7 and Ms near 8.

ML、mb、Ms 由特定週期的波振幅量得。破裂變大時增加的主要是長週期能量，短週期振幅不再增加，規模就頂住（mb 約 6–6.5、ML 約 7、Ms 約 8），所以大地震要用 Mw。

2.6 →
11Seismic intensity (Taiwan CWA scale)震度（中央氣象署新制）

Intensity describes how strongly the ground shakes at a particular site, so it varies from station to station.An earthquake has only one magnitude.The CWA scale (2020) has levels 0–7, with levels 5 and 6 split into weak and strong.Levels up to 4 are based on PGA; higher levels on PGV.

某地搖晃的程度，每個測站不同；規模則一個地震只有一個。2020 年起分 0–7 級，5、6 級再分弱、強，共 10 級；4 級以下依 PGA 分級，PGA ≥ 80 cm/s² 後改依 PGV 分級。

Ch2

強地動

#名詞說明詳細推導
12PGA, PGV and PGD★尖峰地表加速度、速度、位移

The maximum absolute values of the acceleration, velocity and displacement time histories.PGA is controlled by high-frequency, PGV by intermediate-frequency and PGD by low-frequency components.The three peaks usually occur at different times.

加速度、速度、位移歷時的最大絕對值。積分越多次越強調低頻：PGA 由高頻控制、PGV 由中週期、PGD 由長週期，三者通常不在同一時刻出現。軟弱場址的 PGV/PGA 較大。

3.1 →
13Arias intensity Ia★Arias 強度

\(I_a = \frac{\pi}{2g}\int a^2\,dt\) (m/s).The total energy per unit weight absorbed by a set of undamped oscillators covering all frequencies.It reflects amplitude, frequency content and duration of the motion.

\(I_a = \frac{\pi}{2g}\int a^2\,dt\)（m/s），等於一排各種頻率的無阻尼 SDOF 每單位重量吸收的總能量。同時反映振幅（平方）與延時：振幅加倍，Ia 變 4 倍。

3.2 →
14Cumulative absolute velocity, CAV★累積絕對速度

\(CAV = \int|a|\,dt\), the area under the absolute acceleration history.It reflects both amplitude and duration and correlates well with damage and liquefaction.CAV5 accumulates only the parts with |a| ≥ 5 cm/s².

\(CAV = \int|a|\,dt\)，加速度絕對值曲線下的面積（m/s 或 g·s），反映振幅與延時，與損害、液化的相關性好。CAV5 只累積 |a| ≥ 5 cm/s² 的部分，排除弱尾段的影響。

3.2 →CAV5 →
15Bracketed duration Db括號延時新增

The time elapsed between the first and the last exceedance of a threshold acceleration.The threshold is commonly 0.05 g or 5 % of PGA (Bolt 1973).

第一次到最後一次超過門檻加速度之間經過的時間（Bolt 1973）。門檻常用 0.05 g，講義參數表用 5 % PGA；中間變弱也照算，容易被一個晚到的尖峰拉長。

3.2 →
16Significant duration D5–95★顯著延時

The time interval over which 5 % to 95 % of the total Arias intensity is accumulated.It is read from the Husid plot (Trifunac & Brady 1975).An energy-based measure of the duration of strong shaking.

Ia 由 5 % 累積到 95 % 經過的時間，由 Husid 圖讀取（Trifunac & Brady 1975）。以能量定義、不需要門檻，也與振幅無關，是最穩定的延時參數；另有 D5–75。

3.2 →
17Uniform duration Du均勻延時新增

The sum of all time intervals during which the absolute acceleration exceeds a threshold value (Sarma & Casey 1990).It is always less than or equal to the bracketed duration.

|a| 超過門檻的所有時段加總（Sarma & Casey 1990），只算真正在強搖的時間，所以一定 ≤ Db。用絕對門檻時會隨振幅改變。

3.2 →
18Predominant period Tp and mean period Tm★卓越週期與平均週期

Tp: the period at which the 5 %-damped acceleration response spectrum reaches its maximum.Tm = ΣCi²(1/fi)/ΣCi²: the mean period weighted by the squared Fourier amplitudes.Tm uses the frequencies between 0.25 and 20 Hz (Rathje et al. 1998).

Tp：5 % 阻尼加速度反應譜最大值所在的週期（也有人用 Fourier 譜峰值）。Tm = ΣCi²(1/fi)/ΣCi²（0.25–20 Hz，Rathje et al. 1998），用 Fourier 振幅平方加權。軟土與大地震的兩者都較長。

5.3 →3.2 →
19Response spectrum反應譜

A plot of the peak response of a series of SDOF oscillators subjected to the same ground motion.The oscillators have the same damping ratio (usually 5 %) but different natural periods.It describes the effect of the motion on structures.

同一阻尼比（常用 5 %）、不同自然週期的 SDOF 受同一地動作用時的最大反應，對週期作圖；擬加速度 \(S_a = \omega_n^2S_d\)。描述地動對結構的效果。

5.2 →
20Fourier amplitude spectrum★傅立葉振幅譜

A plot of the amplitudes of the harmonic components of a ground motion versus frequency.It is obtained by the Fourier transform.It shows the frequency content of the motion itself.

把地動拆成各頻率的簡諧成分，振幅對頻率作圖，描述地動本身的頻率內容。和反應譜不同，它不含 SDOF 的放大，也看不出哪一個時間發生。

5.1 →
21Near-fault effect★近斷層效應新增

Special characteristics of ground motions within about 15–20 km of the rupture.Forward directivity: a short, large-amplitude velocity pulse, strongest in the fault-normal direction.Fling step: permanent displacement.Hanging-wall effect: hanging-wall sites shake more strongly.Vertical motions are large.

距破裂面約 15–20 km 內的特殊地動：前進方向性造成短延時、大振幅的速度脈衝（垂直斷層走向的分量最大）；fling step 留下永久位移；上盤地動較大；垂直分量大。對長週期結構特別不利。

Ch3

地震波與波速量測

#名詞說明詳細推導
22P waveP 波（壓縮波）

A compressional body wave whose particle motion is parallel to the direction of propagation.It is the fastest wave and travels through both solids and fluids.\(V_p = \sqrt{M/\rho}\).

實體波，質點沿傳播方向一推一拉，改變體積；波速最快，固體與流體都能傳，\(V_p = \sqrt{M/\rho}\)。飽和土的 Vp 被孔隙水拉到約 1500 m/s，反映的是水而不是土骨架。

6.1 →
23S wave (SV and SH)S 波（剪力波）

A shear body wave whose particle motion is perpendicular to the direction of propagation.SV moves in the vertical plane, SH horizontally.It cannot travel through fluids; \(V_s = \sqrt{G/\rho}\).

實體波，質點垂直傳播方向運動，體積不變；SV 在垂直面內、SH 為水平。只能在固體中傳，\(V_s = \sqrt{G/\rho}\)，只反映土骨架的勁度。

6.1 →
24Rayleigh wave★雷利波

A surface wave produced by the interaction of P and SV waves at a free surface.Particles move in a retrograde ellipse.The amplitude decays within about one wavelength of depth.\(c_R \approx 0.87\text{–}0.95\,V_s\).

P 波與 SV 波在自由表面耦合成的表面波，地表質點作逆行橢圓運動，振幅大約在一個波長內衰減。均質半無限域 cR ≈ 0.87–0.95 Vs 且不頻散；層狀土會頻散，是 MASW 的原理。

6.6 →
25Love wave洛夫波

A surface wave formed by SH waves trapped in a soft surface layer over a stiffer half-space.Particle motion is horizontal and perpendicular to the direction of propagation.It is dispersive, with \(V_{s1} < c_L < V_{s2}\).

SH 波在慢層（覆於快層之上）中來回全反射形成的表面波，質點只在水平面內、垂直傳播方向擺動。波速介於上下兩層的 Vs 之間，隨頻率改變（幾何頻散）。

6.7 →
26Constrained modulus M束制模數

The ratio of axial stress to axial strain under zero lateral strain, \(M = \frac{E(1-\nu)}{(1+\nu)(1-2\nu)}\).It governs the P-wave velocity in an infinite medium.A laterally unconfined rod uses E instead.

側向應變為零（一維應變）時軸向應力與軸向應變之比，\(M = \frac{E(1-\nu)}{(1+\nu)(1-2\nu)}\)，控制無限介質中的 P 波速。側向自由的細桿則用 E，波速 \(\sqrt{E/\rho}\)。

6.2 →
27Specific impedance波阻抗

The product ρV, equal to the ratio of stress to particle velocity in a traveling wave (\(\sigma = \rho V\dot u\)).The impedance contrast at an interface controls how much of a wave is reflected and transmitted.

ρV，行進波中應力與質點速度之比（\(\sigma = \rho V\dot u\)）。界面兩側的阻抗差決定波的反射與透射；土與岩盤的阻抗比也決定土層放大的上限。

6.2 →10.7 →
28Wavelength and wave number波長與波數

Wavelength λ = V/f = VT: the distance over which a harmonic wave repeats itself.Wave number k = 2π/λ = ω/V.

\(\lambda = V/f = VT\)；\(k = 2\pi/\lambda = \omega/V\)。波長是判斷尺度的基準：MASW 長波看深、短波看淺；基礎遠小於波長時隨土體一起運動；隔振溝深度以 λR 計。

6.5 →
29Resonant column test (RCT)★共振柱試驗

A soil column is excited in torsion, and the frequency is swept to find the first-mode resonant frequency.Vs is obtained from \(I/I_0 = \beta\tan\beta\), and \(G = \rho V_s^2\).Damping is obtained from free-vibration decay or the half-power bandwidth.Strain range about 10⁻⁴–10⁻¹ %.

以扭轉激振土柱並掃頻，找第一模態共振頻率，由頻率方程式 \(I/I_0 = \beta\tan\beta\) 求 Vs，\(G = \rho V_s^2\)；阻尼由自由衰減或半功率法求得。應變範圍約 10⁻⁴–10⁻¹ %。

9.1 →9.2 →
30Bender element test彎曲元件試驗

Piezoelectric bender elements at the two ends of a specimen send and receive a shear wave.Vs = tip-to-tip distance / travel time.\(G_{max} = \rho V_s^2\).

試體兩端的壓電元件發射與接收剪力波，Vs = 尖端到尖端的距離 / 初達時間，\(G_{max} = \rho V_s^2\)。近場效應會讓初達時間判讀偏早、Vs 偏高。

7.4 →
31Downhole test★下孔試驗

Shear waves generated at the ground surface next to a borehole are recorded by receivers at different depths.The inverse of the slope of the travel time–depth plot gives the Vs of each layer.

在孔口地表激發 S 波，由孔內不同深度的受波器記錄到時；走時–深度圖上各段斜率的倒數就是該層的 Vs，再得 \(G_{max} = \rho V_s^2\)。

7.3 →
32MASW★多頻道表面波分析

Multichannel Analysis of Surface Waves: an array of geophones on the surface records Rayleigh waves.A dispersion curve (phase velocity versus frequency) is extracted and inverted for the Vs profile.It is a non-destructive method that measures small-strain stiffness.

地表一排受波器記錄 Rayleigh 波，求頻散曲線（相速度對頻率）後反算 Vs 剖面。非破壞、成本低，量到的是小應變的 Gmax。

7.2 →
33Vs30 and site class★30 m 平均剪力波速與地盤分類

The time-averaged shear wave velocity of the top 30 m, \(V_{s30} = 30/\sum(h_i/V_{si})\).It is the basis of site classification in NEHRP, Eurocode 8 and the Taiwan seismic design code.

\(V_{s30} = 30/\sum(h_i/V_{si})\)，是走時平均，不是算術平均。NEHRP、Eurocode 8 與我國規範都以它分類；臺灣 ≥ 270 m/s 為第一類、180–270 為第二類、< 180 為第三類。

振動理論

#名詞說明詳細推導
34Single degree of freedom (SDOF) system★單自由度系統

A system whose motion can be described by a single coordinate.It consists of a mass, a spring and a dashpot: \(m\ddot u + c\dot u + ku = p(t)\).

只需一個座標就能描述運動的系統，由質量、彈簧、阻尼器組成：\(m\ddot u + c\dot u + ku = p(t)\)。共振柱、土層、機器基礎、隔震建築都能化成它。

4.1 →
35Natural frequency★自然頻率

The frequency of undamped free vibration: \(\omega_n = \sqrt{k/m}\) (rad/s), \(f_n = \omega_n/2\pi\), \(T_n = 1/f_n\).With damping, the system vibrates at \(\omega_D = \omega_n\sqrt{1-\xi^2}\).

無阻尼自由振動的頻率 \(\omega_n = \sqrt{k/m}\)（rad/s），\(f = \omega_n/2\pi\)、\(T = 1/f\)。有阻尼時以 \(\omega_D = \omega_n\sqrt{1-\xi^2}\) 振動。

4.2 →
36Critical damping coefficient★臨界阻尼係數

The smallest damping coefficient for which a displaced system returns to equilibrium without oscillating.\(c_c = 2m\omega_n = 2\sqrt{km}\).ξ < 1 is underdamped, ξ = 1 critically damped and ξ > 1 overdamped.

使特徵方程式出現重根、系統不振盪且最快回到平衡的阻尼係數：\(c_c = 2m\omega_n = 2\sqrt{km}\)。ξ < 1 次阻尼（衰減振盪）、ξ = 1 臨界阻尼、ξ > 1 超阻尼（不振盪、回復較慢）。

4.3 →
37Damping ratio ξ★阻尼比

\(\xi = c/c_c\), a dimensionless measure of damping.In energy terms, \(\xi = W_D/(4\pi W_S)\).WD: energy dissipated per cycle; WS: maximum strain energy.

\(\xi = c/c_c\)，無因次。能量定義 \(\xi = W_D/(4\pi W_S)\)（每圈消散的能量 / 最大應變能），可推廣到土壤的遲滯迴圈。單元 8、9 改用 D 表示阻尼比。

4.3 →4.8 →
38Logarithmic decrement δ★對數遞減

The natural logarithm of the ratio of two successive peak amplitudes of free vibration.\(\delta = \ln(u_n/u_{n+1}) = 2\pi\xi/\sqrt{1-\xi^2} \approx 2\pi\xi\).It is used to measure damping.

自由振動相鄰兩峰值比的自然對數：\(\delta = \ln(u_n/u_{n+1}) = 2\pi\xi/\sqrt{1-\xi^2} \approx 2\pi\xi\)。隔 m 圈取 \(\delta = \frac1m\ln(u_n/u_{n+m})\) 較準。

4.9 →
39Transient and steady-state response暫態與穩態反應

Under harmonic loading, total response = transient response + steady-state response.Transient: the homogeneous solution, decaying with \(e^{-\xi\omega_n t}\) and determined by the initial conditions.Steady state: the particular solution at the forcing frequency, with amplitude ρsD and phase lag φ.

受簡諧外力時，總反應 = 暫態（齊次解，以 ωD 振動、按 \(e^{-\xi\omega t}\) 衰減，常數由初始條件決定）+ 穩態（特解，以外力頻率振動，振幅 ρsD、相位落後 φ）。

4.4 →4.7 →
40Resonance★共振

Forcing frequency = natural frequency (β = 1).The spring and inertia forces cancel, so the applied load is balanced by the damping force alone.The dynamic magnification factor equals 1/(2ξ), and the displacement lags the load by 90°.Without damping, the amplitude grows linearly with time, by π times the static displacement per cycle.With damping, it still takes several cycles to build up to the steady state.

外力頻率等於自然頻率（β = 1）時，彈簧力與慣性力互相抵消，外力只由阻尼力平衡，振幅放大為 1/(2ξ) 倍、位移落後 90°。無阻尼時振幅隨時間線性成長，每圈增加 π 倍靜態變位；有阻尼時也要好幾圈才累積到穩態。

4.5 →4.7 →
41Dynamic magnification factor D★動態放大因子

Ratio of the steady-state amplitude to the static displacement p0/k:\(D = 1/\sqrt{(1-\beta^2)^2 + (2\xi\beta)^2}\).β ≪ 1: stiffness-controlled, D ≈ 1.β = 1: damping-controlled, D = 1/(2ξ).β ≫ 1: inertia-controlled, D ≈ 1/β².Peak \(D_{max} = 1/(2\xi\sqrt{1-\xi^2})\) at \(\beta = \sqrt{1-2\xi^2}\), slightly below resonance.

穩態振幅 / 靜態變位：\(D = 1/\sqrt{(1-\beta^2)^2 + (2\xi\beta)^2}\)。β ≪ 1 勁度控制（D ≈ 1）、β = 1 阻尼控制（D = 1/2ξ）、β ≫ 1 慣性控制（D ≈ 1/β²）；峰值在 \(\beta = \sqrt{1-2\xi^2}\)，\(D_{max} = 1/(2\xi\sqrt{1-\xi^2})\)。

4.4 →4.5 →
42Half-power bandwidth method★半功率頻寬法

A method to estimate damping from a frequency response curve.\(\xi \approx (f_2-f_1)/(f_2+f_1)\).f1 and f2 are the frequencies at which the amplitude equals 1/√2 of the peak amplitude.

在頻率響應曲線上找振幅等於峰值 1/√2 的兩個頻率 f1、f2：\(\xi \approx (f_2-f_1)/(f_2+f_1)\)。只需要曲線的形狀，不需要外力大小；大應變時曲線傾斜會高估阻尼。

43Transmissibility TR1, TR2★傳遞率

TR1: the force transmitted to the base / the applied force. TR2: the mass motion / the base motion.\(TR_1 = TR_2 = D\sqrt{1+(2\xi\beta)^2}\).Vibration isolation (TR1 = TR2 < 1) requires β > √2.

TR1 = 傳到地面的力 / 外力，TR2 = 質量位移 / 地面位移，兩者是同一個函數：\(TR_1 = TR_2 = D\sqrt{1+(2\xi\beta)^2}\)。β > √2 時傳遞率 < 1 才有隔振效果，所以支承要軟；在隔振區阻尼越大，傳遞越多。

44Secant shear modulus Gsec and Gmax★割線剪力模數與小應變剪力模數

49Backbone curve and Masing rules★骨幹曲線與 Masing 準則

50Hyperbolic model and reference strain γr★雙曲線模型與參考應變

52Transfer function轉換函數

The ratio of an output motion (e.g. ground surface) to an input motion (e.g. bedrock) as a function of frequency.For a damped uniform layer on rigid rock, \(|F| \approx 1/\sqrt{\cos^2(\omega H/V_s) + (\xi\omega H/V_s)^2}\).

54Impedance ratio and radiation damping★阻抗比與輻射阻尼

56Nonlinear (time-domain) analysis非線性（時間域）分析

The equation of motion is integrated step by step in the time domain with a nonlinear stress–strain model.The stiffness is updated at every step.It can model large strains and permanent deformation.With a pore pressure model, it can also model liquefaction.

以非線性 τ–γ 模型在時間域逐步積分運動方程式，每一步更新勁度；可模擬大應變與永久變形，配合孔隙水壓模型可做液化分析。代價是參數多、要檢查數值穩定與邊界。

57Total stress vs effective stress analysis★總應力分析與有效應力分析

Total stress analysis: uses G and ξ without computing pore pressures (e.g. equivalent linear analysis).Effective stress analysis: couples a pore pressure model.It predicts excess pore pressure, softening, liquefaction and permanent deformation.

59Flow liquefaction流動液化

Occurs when the static shear stress exceeds the residual (steady-state) strength of the liquefied soil.It produces sudden flow failures with very large deformations.Only loose, contractive soils above the steady state line are susceptible.

靜態剪應力大於液化後殘餘強度時發生，破壞突然、變形很大，地震停了還可能繼續（流滑）。只發生在狀態位於臨界狀態線上方的鬆、收縮性土。

60Cyclic mobility★反覆流動性

Occurs when the static shear stress is smaller than the residual strength.Cyclic loading raises the pore pressure and accumulates deformation cycle by cycle.ru may reach 1 momentarily at stress reversals, but dilation restores strength.Deformations are therefore limited (e.g. lateral spreading).

靜剪應力小於殘餘強度，反覆載重使孔隙水壓逐圈上升、變形逐圈累積，鬆砂到密砂都可能發生。剪應力反轉的瞬間 ru 可達 1，但繼續剪動時土會膨脹、強度回升，所以變形有限（側潰、沉陷），地震停就停。
