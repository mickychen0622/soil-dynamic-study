名詞解釋

作答順序：一句定義 → 公式或判別方式 → 一兩個關鍵性質或實例。2025 年的格式是「briefly describe in English/Chinese what you know about…」，每題 4 分。

#名詞說明詳細說明
1Plate tectonics and plate boundaries板塊構造與板塊邊界

The lithosphere (crust + uppermost mantle, about 100 km thick) is broken into a dozen or so plates that move a few cm per year over the asthenosphere.Divergent boundary: plates move apart; normal faults; shallow, smaller earthquakes (mid-ocean ridges, East African Rift).Convergent boundary: subduction or collision; reverse (thrust) faults; every Mw 9+ earthquake has occurred in a subduction zone.Transform boundary: plates slide past each other; strike-slip faults (San Andreas).About 80 % of large earthquakes occur in the Circum-Pacific belt.

岩石圈（地殼＋最上部地函，約 100 km）分成十幾塊板塊，在軟流圈上每年移動數公分。張裂邊界→正斷層、淺源較小地震；聚合邊界（隱沒或碰撞）→逆斷層，Mw 9 以上都在隱沒帶；錯動邊界→走向滑移。全球約八成大地震在環太平洋地震帶。

2.1 →
2Induced earthquake誘發地震

An earthquake triggered by human activity: loading (reservoir impoundment), unloading (mining, excavation) or fluid injection (wastewater disposal, geothermal).Mechanism: effective stress. Injection raises the pore pressure u, so \(\tau_f = c + \mu(\sigma_n - u)\) drops, and a fault that is already near failure slips.Examples: Koyna reservoir, India (1967, M 6.3); Oklahoma after 2009; Pohang, Korea (2017, Mw 5.5).

人為活動引發的地震：加載（水庫蓄水）、卸載（採礦、開挖）、注入流體（廢水回注、地熱）。機制是有效應力：孔隙水壓上升使斷層強度下降，原本接近臨界的斷層就滑動。

2.2 →
3Elastic rebound theory彈性回跳理論

Reid (1910), from the 1906 San Francisco earthquake: relative plate motion builds up elastic strain in the rock on both sides of a locked fault.When the shear stress exceeds the fault strength, the fault slips suddenly; the rock rebounds and the stored strain energy is released as seismic waves.Strain accumulates over hundreds of years and is released in seconds to minutes; slip per event ≈ plate rate × recurrence interval.

斷層被摩擦鎖住，兩側岩體累積彈性應變；剪應力超過強度時突然滑動，岩體彈回，應變能以地震波釋放，然後重新累積。慢慢累積、瞬間釋放；一次錯動量 ≈ 板塊速率 × 再現週期。

2.4 →
4Hypocenter (focus) and epicenter震源與震央

The hypocenter (focus) is the point inside the earth where fault rupture starts.The epicenter is the point on the ground surface directly above it; the distance between them is the focal depth h.For a large rupture, the closest distance to the rupture plane (Rrup) is more meaningful than the distance to either point.

震源是斷層開始破裂的地下點；震央是震源正上方的地表點，兩者距離為震源深度。大地震破裂面很長，工程上改用到破裂面的最短距離 Rrup。

2.3 →
5Strike, dip and rake走向、傾角、滑移角

Strike φ: the azimuth (clockwise from north) of the line where the fault plane meets a horizontal surface.Dip δ: the angle (0–90°) between the fault plane and the horizontal, measured perpendicular to strike; by convention the plane dips to the right when facing the strike direction.Rake λ: the slip direction of the hanging wall relative to the footwall, measured on the fault plane from the strike direction: +90° reverse, −90° normal, 0° left-lateral, 180° right-lateral.

走向：斷層面與水平面交線的方位，由北順時針量。傾角：斷層面與水平面的夾角，在垂直走向的剖面上量；慣例面向走向時往右手邊傾。滑移角：上盤相對下盤的滑動方向，90° 逆斷層、−90° 正斷層、0° 左移、180° 右移。

2.3 →互動圖 →
6Hanging wall and footwall上盤與下盤

For an inclined fault, the block above the fault plane is the hanging wall; the block below it is the footwall.Normal and reverse faults are told apart only by whether the hanging wall moves down or up.A vertical fault has no hanging wall or footwall.

傾斜斷層面上方的岩塊是上盤、下方是下盤（站在斷層面上，頭頂那塊是上盤）。正、逆斷層只看上盤往下或往上；垂直斷層面沒有上下盤之分。

2.3 →
7Normal fault正斷層

Motion: the hanging wall slides down the fault plane relative to the footwall (rake ≈ −90°).Cause: horizontal extension, σ1 vertical and σ3 horizontal, so the crust gets longer.Typical of divergent boundaries (mid-ocean ridges, rifts); the beach ball has a white (dilatational) center.

上盤沿斷層面往下滑；水平伸張（σ1 垂直、σ3 水平），地殼變長。常見於洋脊、裂谷；海灘球中心為白色。

2.3 →
8Reverse fault★逆斷層

Motion: the hanging wall is pushed up over the footwall (rake ≈ +90°).Cause: horizontal compression, σ1 horizontal and σ3 vertical, so the crust gets shorter.A reverse fault dipping less than about 45° is a thrust fault.Typical of convergent boundaries (subduction zones, collision belts); the beach ball has a shaded (compressional) center.Example: the east-dipping Chelungpu thrust fault, 1999 Chi-Chi earthquake (Mw 7.6), about 100 km of surface rupture; the hanging wall rose about 10 m at the Shih-Kang dam.

上盤被推上去、爬到下盤上方；水平壓縮（σ1 水平、σ3 垂直），地殼縮短。傾角小於約 45° 的叫逆衝斷層。常見於隱沒帶與碰撞帶（臺灣西部麓山帶）；海灘球中心為陰影。例：1999 集集地震的車籠埔斷層，向東傾的逆衝斷層，地表破裂約 100 km，石岡壩處上盤抬升約 10 m。

9Strike-slip fault走向滑移（平移）斷層

Motion: the two blocks slide horizontally past each other along the strike (rake ≈ 0° or 180°).Cause: σ1 and σ3 both horizontal, σ2 vertical.Sense: if the block across the fault moves to the right it is right-lateral, to the left left-lateral; the answer is the same from either side.Typical of transform boundaries, e.g. the right-lateral San Andreas fault.

兩側沿走向水平錯開；σ1、σ3 都在水平面上、σ2 垂直。看對面那塊往右移是右移、往左移是左移，站哪一側看都一樣。常見於錯動邊界，如聖安地列斯斷層（右移）。

2.3 →
10Focal mechanism (beach ball)斷層面解（海灘球）

A plot of P-wave first motions projected onto the lower focal hemisphere.Shaded quadrants are compressional (contain the T axis); white quadrants are dilatational (contain the P axis).The two boundaries are nodal planes; one of them is the fault plane, identified from aftershocks or surface rupture.White center → normal; shaded center → reverse; four-leaf cross → strike-slip.

把各站 P 波初動投影到震源下半球。陰影＝壓縮象限（含 T 軸），白色＝伸張象限（含 P 軸）。兩條分界是節面，其中一個才是斷層面，要靠餘震或地表破裂判斷。中心白→正斷層、中心陰影→逆斷層、四葉十字→走向滑移。

2.3 →
11Surface fault rupture地表破裂（斷層錯動）

Permanent offset of the ground surface where fault slip reaches the surface.Shaking can be resisted by design, but a structure straddling the fault can hardly resist the offset: avoid the fault or design to accommodate the movement.Through a soil cover the rupture does not appear directly above the bedrock fault: the fault controls the offset, the soil controls the location; the thicker the soil, the larger the shift.

斷層錯動延伸到地表造成的永久錯位。震動可以靠設計抵抗，跨越斷層的錯動幾乎不行，只能避開或設計成可容許錯動。有覆土時地表破裂不在岩盤斷層的投影上：斷層決定落差，土壤決定位置，覆土越厚偏離越多。例：石岡壩溢洪道被剪斷。

2.4 →
12Active fault活動斷層

A fault with evidence of movement in the recent geologic past (typically the last 100,000 years) that is likely to rupture again.Taiwan: Type I active within 10,000 years, Type II within 100,000 years (about 36 faults on the published map).Engineering use: Active Fault Geologically Sensitive Areas (site investigation required before development) and the source-to-site distance in seismic hazard analysis.

未來可能再錯動的斷層，一般以近 10 萬年內有活動證據者認定。臺灣分第一類（近 1 萬年）與第二類（近 10 萬年），目前約 36 條；依地質法劃定活動斷層地質敏感區，開發前須做地質調查；也是危害度分析中距離的依據。

2.4 →
13Seismic intensity (Taiwan CWA scale)震度（中央氣象署新制）

Intensity describes how strongly the ground shakes at a particular site, so it differs from station to station (distance, path, site); an earthquake has only one magnitude.The CWA scale (2020) has levels 0–7, with 5 and 6 split into weak and strong (10 levels).Levels 0–4 are assigned by PGA; once PGA ≥ 80 cm/s², the level is assigned by PGV, because building damage correlates better with velocity.

某地搖晃的程度，每站不同；規模一個地震只有一個。2020 新制 0–7 級，5、6 級分弱、強，共 10 級；4 級以下依 PGA，PGA ≥ 80 cm/s² 後改依 PGV（損害與速度相關性較好）。

2.5 →分級表 →
14Magnitude (ML, mb, Ms)規模

A single number for the size of an earthquake at its source, on a logarithmic scale.The early scales are defined from the amplitude of a particular wave at a particular period: ML (local, Richter) from waves of about 0.1–1 s, mb from about 1 s P waves, Ms from about 20 s surface waves.Because they read a fixed period, they saturate for large earthquakes.

描述震源大小的單一數值（對數尺度）。早期規模由特定週期波的振幅定義：ML 約 0.1–1 s、mb 約 1 s 的 P 波、Ms 約 20 s 的表面波；因為只看固定週期，大地震時會飽和。

2.6 →
15Seismic moment M0 and moment magnitude Mw地震矩與地震矩規模

Seismic moment \(M_0 = \mu AD\) (rigidity × rupture area × average slip) measures the physical size of the rupture.\(M_w = \tfrac23(\log M_0 - 9.05)\) with M0 in N·m, or \((\log M_0 - 16.05)/1.5\) in dyne·cm.One unit of Mw = 101.5 ≈ 32 times in M0 (and in radiated energy).Mw does not saturate.

\(M_0 = \mu AD\) 直接反映破裂大小；Mw 由 M0 計算，常數要配合單位。ΔMw = 1 時 M0（與能量）差約 32 倍；Mw 不會飽和。

2.6 →練習 1.1
16Magnitude saturation規模飽和

Magnitudes based on the wave amplitude at a fixed period stop increasing for large earthquakes.Reason: a larger rupture (longer, with a longer rupture time) adds mainly long-period energy, so the short-period amplitude no longer grows.mb saturates near 6–6.5, ML near 7 and Ms near 8; large earthquakes are rated by Mw.

破裂變大時增加的主要是長週期能量，短週期振幅不再增加，規模就頂住（mb 約 6–6.5、ML 約 7、Ms 約 8），所以大地震用 Mw。集集 ML 7.3、Mw 7.6。

2.6 →
17Source-to-site distanceHW1震源到工址的距離

Epicentral distance Repi: to the epicenter, along the surface. Hypocentral distance \(R_{hyp} = \sqrt{R_{epi}^2 + h^2}\).For large ruptures, point distances are misleading: use Rrup (closest distance to the rupture plane) or Rjb (closest horizontal distance to its surface projection).Ground-motion parameters attenuate with distance (HW1, Chi-Chi records vs Rrup).

震央距沿地表量到震央；震源距再計入震源深度。大地震的破裂面長達數十公里，改用到破裂面的最短距離 Rrup，或到破裂面地表投影的最短水平距離 Rjb。HW1 以 Rrup 看地動參數的衰減。

解題公式

本章公式不多：A 斷層何時滑動，B 地震有多大，C 某地搖多大，D 距離。

A　斷層力學

#名稱公式與說明推導／練習
A1Fault strength (effective stress)斷層強度（有效應力）
\[ \tau_f = c + \mu(\sigma_n - u),\qquad \text{slip when } \tau \ge \tau_f \]

μ here is the friction coefficient. Raising u lowers the strength and can trigger slip.

此處 μ 是摩擦係數。例：σn = 50 MPa、τ = 28 MPa、μ = 0.6、c = 0 → 0.6(50 − u) = 28 → u = 3.33 MPa 就會滑動。

2.2 →互動圖 →
A2Slip per event (elastic rebound)單次錯動量與再現週期
\[ D \approx v_{plate}\,T_r \]

A rough estimate: real faults are irregular, so hazard analysis treats recurrence probabilistically.

粗估用：2 cm/年 × 300 年 ≈ 6 m；錯動 4.5 m、速率 3 cm/年 → 再現週期約 150 年。

2.4 →

B　地震矩、規模與能量

#名稱公式與說明推導／練習
B1Seismic moment地震矩
\[ M_0 = \mu AD,\qquad A = L\times W \]

μ ≈ 3×10¹⁰ N/m² = 3×10¹¹ dyne/cm². 1 N·m = 10⁷ dyne·cm; 1 km = 10⁵ cm.

μ 是剛性模數；單位全用 SI 得 N·m，全用 cgs 得 dyne·cm，不要混用。

2.6 →練習 1.1
B2Moment magnitude地震矩規模
\[ \begin{aligned} &M_w = \tfrac23(\log M_0 - 9.05)\quad (\text{N}\cdot\text{m}) \\ &M_w = \tfrac23(\log M_0 - 16.05)\quad (\text{dyne}\cdot\text{cm}) \end{aligned} \]

Use the constant that matches the unit of M0; both give the same Mw.

學生講義另有 9.1／16.1 一套，兩套不能混搭（集集用 9.05 得 7.61、用 9.1 得 7.57）。

2.6 →計算機 →練習 1.1
B3Magnitude difference and moment ratio規模差與地震矩比
\[ \frac{M_{0,1}}{M_{0,2}} = 10^{1.5\,\Delta M_w},\qquad \Delta M_w = \frac{\log(M_{0,1}/M_{0,2})}{1.5} \]

ΔMw = 0.2 → 2.0 times; 1 → 31.6 times; 2 → 1000 times.

規模差 0.2 就是 2 倍，差 1 是 31.6 倍（不是 10 倍）。

2.6 →練習 1.1(c)
B4Radiated energy輻射能量
\[ E_s \approx \frac{M_0}{2\times10^4},\qquad \frac{E_{s,1}}{E_{s,2}} \approx 10^{1.5\,\Delta M_w} \]

Energy is roughly proportional to M0, so one magnitude unit ≈ 32 times the energy.

能量約與 M0 成正比，所以規模加 1、能量約 32 倍。

計算題題型與解題步驟

本章的計算都很短，失分多半在單位與常數。每一步寫「公式 = 代入 = 結果（單位）」。

題型典型已知解題步驟練習
① 地震矩與 Mw模擬考 1.1(a)(b)破裂長 L、寬 W、平均滑移 D、剛性模數 μ
  1. 先決定單位系統：全 SI（m、Pa）或全 cgs（cm、dyne/cm²）
  2. A = L × W（km → m 乘 10³、→ cm 乘 10⁵）
  3. M0 = μAD
  4. Mw = ⅔(log M0 − 9.05)（N·m）或 − 16.05（dyne·cm）
  5. 答案寫到小數一位或兩位，必要時與已知地震比較
1.12.6 例
② 規模差與倍數模擬考 1.1(c)兩個 Mw，或地震矩（能量）比
  1. M0 比 = 101.5ΔMw
  2. 能量比近似相同（Es ∝ M0）
  3. 反求：ΔMw = log(比值)/1.5
  4. 寫一句結論：ΔMw = 1 約 32 倍，不是 10 倍
1.12 重點
③ 判斷斷層類型模擬考 1.1(d)傾角與上盤動向、滑移角，或海灘球
  1. 先確認有上下盤（傾斜斷層）
  2. 上盤上移 → 逆斷層（傾角 < 45° 為逆衝）；上盤下移 → 正斷層；水平錯開 → 走向滑移（看對面判斷左、右移）
  3. 或由滑移角：+90° 逆、−90° 正、0° 左移、180° 右移
  4. 或由海灘球：中心陰影逆、中心白正、四葉十字走向滑移
  5. 寫出應力狀態：逆 σ3 垂直（水平壓縮）、正 σ1 垂直（水平伸張）、走向滑移 σ2 垂直
1.12.3 互動圖
④ 判定震度中央氣象署新制某站 PGA、PGV（PGA 給 g 時先換算）
  1. PGA 換成 cm/s²（1 g = 981 cm/s²）
  2. PGA < 80 → 依 PGA 查 0–4 級
  3. PGA ≥ 80 → 依 PGV 查：< 15 判 4 級，15–30 5 弱、30–50 5 強、50–80 6 弱、80–140 6 強、≥ 140 為 7 級

例：PGA = 120 cm/s²、PGV = 35 cm/s → 5 強；PGA = 0.252 g = 247 cm/s²、PGV = 21.4 cm/s → 5 弱。

簡答題的關鍵觀念

本章最常見的是比較題。每題先寫結論，再寫一兩句理由或實例。

#問題答題重點詳細說明
1Magnitude vs. intensity規模與震度有何不同？

Magnitude measures the size (energy release) of the earthquake at the source: one value per earthquake.Intensity measures how strongly the ground shakes at a site: it differs from station to station and depends on distance, travel path and site conditions.Magnitude comes from wave amplitudes or M0; Taiwan's intensity comes from recorded PGA and PGV.

規模描述震源，一個地震一個值；震度描述某地的搖晃，每站不同，受距離、路徑與場址影響。規模由波振幅或地震矩算出；臺灣震度由 PGA、PGV 判定。

2.5 →
2Compare normal, reverse and strike-slip faults三種斷層的比較

Normal: hanging wall moves down; σ1 vertical; horizontal extension; divergent boundaries (ridges, rifts).Reverse (thrust): hanging wall moves up; σ3 vertical; horizontal shortening; convergent boundaries (subduction, collision, western Taiwan).Strike-slip: horizontal motion along strike; σ2 vertical; transform boundaries (San Andreas).The vertical principal stress is the one that differs: σ1, σ3 or σ2.

正斷層：上盤下移、σ1 垂直、伸張、張裂邊界。逆斷層：上盤上移、σ3 垂直、縮短、聚合邊界。走向滑移：水平錯動、σ2 垂直、錯動邊界。記法：看垂直方向是哪一個主應力。

2.3 →2.1 →
3Why do ML, mb, Ms saturate but Mw does not?為什麼 Mw 不會飽和？

ML, mb and Ms are read from the amplitude at a fixed period (about 0.1–1 s, 1 s, 20 s).A larger rupture adds mainly long-period energy; the amplitude at that period stops growing, so the magnitude levels off (mb 6–6.5, ML ≈ 7, Ms ≈ 8).Mw is computed from M0 = μAD, a physical measure of the whole rupture, so it keeps increasing with rupture size.Example: Chi-Chi ML 7.3 vs Mw 7.6; 2024 Hualien ML 7.2 vs Mw 7.4.

早期規模只看固定週期的振幅；破裂變大時增加的是長週期能量，該週期的振幅不再增加，規模就頂住。Mw 由整個破裂的 μAD 算出，不受週期限制，所以不飽和；大地震時 ML 與 Mw 差距變大。

4Why does Taiwan have so many earthquakes?臺灣為什麼多震？

Taiwan lies on the convergent boundary between the Philippine Sea plate and the Eurasian plate (about 8 cm/yr toward the northwest).Two subduction zones of opposite polarity: in the northeast the Philippine Sea plate subducts northward (Ryukyu Trench); in the south the Eurasian plate subducts eastward (Manila Trench).In between, the Luzon arc collides with the continental margin, building the Central Range; thrust faults in the western foothills (e.g. Chelungpu) absorb the shortening.

臺灣位於菲律賓海板塊與歐亞板塊的聚合邊界（約 8 cm/年往西北）。東北菲律賓海板塊向北隱沒、南部歐亞板塊向東隱沒，中間呂宋島弧碰撞大陸邊緣；西部麓山帶的逆衝斷層吸收縮短量，集集地震即屬此類。

2.1 →
5How can fluid injection or a reservoir trigger an earthquake?注水、水庫為什麼會引發地震？

The fault strength follows Mohr–Coulomb with effective stress: \(\tau_f = c + \mu(\sigma_n - u)\).Injected water raises u, lowers σn − u and τf; when τf falls below the existing tectonic shear stress, the fault slips.A reservoir adds normal stress, but water seeping into the fault raises u, which often dominates.The same mechanism, with u generated by cyclic shear in undrained soil, causes liquefaction.

斷層強度遵守有效應力原理。注水使孔隙水壓上升、強度下降，低於原有的構造剪應力就滑動。水庫的水重雖增加正應力，但水滲入斷層後孔壓上升的效果常較大。同一機制換成土壤就是液化。

2.2 →
6Plates move only cm/yr; why does a fault slip meters at once?板塊每年只動幾公分，為何一次錯動數公尺？

Elastic rebound: the fault is locked by friction, so plate motion is stored as elastic strain for hundreds of years.When the stress exceeds the strength, all the accumulated displacement is released in one slip within seconds to minutes.Slip ≈ plate rate × recurrence interval (2 cm/yr × 300 yr ≈ 6 m).

斷層被鎖住，板塊運動以彈性應變累積數百年，超過強度時一次釋放。錯動量 ≈ 速率 × 再現週期。

7Why does the CWA scale use PGV for level 5 and above?新制震度為何 5 級以上看 PGV？

Building damage correlates better with ground velocity than with peak acceleration.A short-period acceleration spike can give a high PGA with little damage; such records (PGA ≥ 80 cm/s² but PGV < 15 cm/s) are rated level 4.

建物損害與速度的相關性比加速度好；短週期的尖峰加速度 PGA 雖大、損害不大，所以 PGA ≥ 80 但 PGV < 15 cm/s 仍判 4 級。

2.5 →
8How should engineers deal with active faults?工程上如何面對活動斷層？

Permanent offset across a fault cannot be resisted by strength: avoid crossing it, or design to accommodate it (sliding bearings, enough seat length).Through soil, the surface rupture is offset from the mapped bedrock fault, more so for thick soil and gentle dips: do a site investigation instead of relying on a fixed-width zone.Taiwan designates Active Fault Geologically Sensitive Areas; distance to the fault also enters seismic hazard analysis.

跨越斷層的永久錯動無法用強度抵抗，只能避開或設計成可容許錯動。有覆土時地表破裂會偏離地圖上的斷層線，覆土厚、傾角緩時偏得更多，所以開發前要做場址調查；臺灣劃定活動斷層地質敏感區。

2.4 →
9How do we know the outer core is liquid?怎麼知道外核是液態？

\(V_s = \sqrt{G/\rho}\); a liquid has G = 0, so S waves cannot pass through it.Stations about 104°–140° from the epicenter receive no direct S waves (the S-wave shadow zone), while P waves still pass.

液體 G = 0、Vs = 0，S 波過不了；離震央約 104°–140° 收不到直達 S 波（陰影區），P 波仍可通過。

10How do you read a beach ball, and which plane is the fault?海灘球怎麼讀？哪個節面是斷層？

Look at the center: shaded → reverse, white → normal, four-leaf cross → strike-slip.Both nodal planes produce the same pattern, so the fault plane must be chosen with other data: aftershock distribution or surface rupture.

看中心：陰影逆斷層、白色正斷層、四葉十字走向滑移。兩個節面給出同一顆球，要靠餘震分布或地表破裂決定哪一個是斷層面。

常見陷阱

  • 規模 ≠ 震度：一個地震一個規模，震度每站不同；新制 PGA ≥ 80 cm/s² 後看 PGV。
  • ΔMw = 1 是 32 倍：101.5 = 31.6，不是 10 倍；ΔMw = 0.2 已是 2 倍。
  • Mw 常數配單位：N·m 用 9.05、dyne·cm 用 16.05；另一套 9.1／16.1 不可混搭。
  • 單位換算：1 N·m = 10⁷ dyne·cm；1 km = 10³ m = 10⁵ cm，面積 1 km² = 10¹⁰ cm²。
  • 逆斷層的應力：水平壓縮，σ1 水平、σ3 垂直；正斷層才是 σ1 垂直。
  • 逆斷層與逆衝：逆衝斷層是傾角小於約 45° 的逆斷層，不是另一種動向。
  • 右移、左移看對面：站在哪一側看都一樣，看對面那塊往哪移。
  • 符號撞名：本章 δ 是傾角、D 是滑移量、μ 是摩擦係數或剛性模數，和單元 4 的 δ、D 不同。
  • 地殼不是 100 km 厚：陸殼約 30–70 km、洋殼約 5–10 km；約 100 km 是岩石圈。
  • 大地震用 Rrup：破裂面長數十公里，震央距不能代表工址到震源的距離。
