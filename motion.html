名詞解釋

作答順序：一句定義 → 公式 → 一兩個關鍵性質。2025 年的格式是「briefly describe in English/Chinese what you know about…」，每題 4 分。

#名詞說明詳細說明
1PGA, PGV and PGDHW1尖峰地表加速度、速度、位移

The maximum absolute values of the ground acceleration, velocity and displacement time histories.Velocity and displacement are obtained by integrating the recorded acceleration; integration divides by iω, so PGA is controlled by high frequencies, PGV by intermediate periods and PGD by long periods.The three peaks usually occur at different times; soft sites have a larger PGV/PGA ratio (longer-period motion).

加速度、速度、位移歷時的最大絕對值。積分越多次越強調低頻：PGA 由高頻控制、PGV 由中週期、PGD 由長週期，三個峰值通常不在同一時刻。軟弱場址 PGA 可能較小，但 PGV/PGA 較大。

3.1 →
2RotD100HW1旋轉取最大值

The maximum of a parameter over all rotation angles θ = 0–179° of the two horizontal components, \(a_\theta = a_1\cos\theta + a_2\sin\theta\).PGARotD100 equals the peak of the resultant horizontal vector, so it is never smaller than the larger of the two components.

把兩個水平分量旋轉 0–179°，取各角度中的最大值。PGARotD100 等於水平合成向量的最大值，一定 ≥ 兩分量中較大者，不是「兩分量取大」。

HW1 B →
3Arias intensity IaHW1Arias 強度

\(I_a = \frac{\pi}{2g}\int_0^{t_d} a^2(t)\,dt\), in m/s.Physically, the total energy per unit weight absorbed by a set of undamped SDOF oscillators covering all natural frequencies.It combines amplitude (squared) and duration: doubling the amplitude gives 4 × Ia; it correlates better than PGA with cumulative damage such as liquefaction and slope sliding.

\(I_a = \frac{\pi}{2g}\int a^2dt\)（m/s），等於一排各種頻率的無阻尼 SDOF 每單位重量吸收的總能量。同時反映振幅（平方）與延時：振幅加倍，Ia 變 4 倍。與液化、邊坡滑動等累積損害的相關性比 PGA 好。

3.2 →
4Cumulative absolute velocity, CAV★HW1累積絕對速度

The time integral of the absolute acceleration: \(CAV = \int_0^{t_d}|a(t)|\,dt\), i.e. the area under the |a(t)| curve (m/s, or g·s).It grows with both amplitude (linearly) and duration, so two records with the same PGA can have very different CAV.It correlates well with structural damage and liquefaction.A long, weak tail keeps increasing CAV; CAV5 (Kramer & Mitchell 2006) counts only the parts with |a| ≥ 5 cm/s² to remove this effect.

加速度絕對值對時間的積分，即 |a(t)| 曲線下的面積（m/s 或 g·s）。隨振幅（一次方）與延時增加，PGA 相同的紀錄 CAV 可差很多；與損害、液化相關性好。很長的弱尾段會一直加大 CAV，CAV5 只累積 |a| ≥ 5 cm/s² 的部分。

3.2 →CAV5 →
5CAV5門檻累積絕對速度

\(CAV_5 = \int\langle\chi\rangle|a|\,dt\), with ⟨χ⟩ = 1 when |a| ≥ 5 cm/s² and 0 otherwise (Kramer & Mitchell 2006).It excludes the small-amplitude tail that does not affect the soil, so it predicts liquefaction and settlement better than CAV.

只累積 |a| ≥ 5 cm/s² 的部分；排除對土壤沒影響的弱尾段，所以和液化、沉陷的相關性比 CAV 更好。

3.2 →
6Husid plotHusid 圖

A plot of the normalized cumulative Arias intensity \(H(t) = \int_0^t a^2d\tau \big/ \int_0^{t_d}a^2d\tau\) versus time.It is an S-shaped curve rising from 0 to 1; the steep part is the strong-shaking phase.Significant durations D5–75 and D5–95 are read from it.

Ia 累積比例對時間作圖，從 0 爬到 1 的 S 形曲線，陡的那段就是強震段；顯著延時由它讀取。

3.2 →
7Significant duration D5–95★HW1顯著延時

The time interval during which 5 % to 95 % of the total Arias intensity is accumulated: \(D_{5\text{–}95} = t_{95} - t_5\), read from the Husid plot (Trifunac & Brady 1975).It is energy-based and needs no acceleration threshold.It is independent of amplitude: scaling the record changes Ia but not H(t).D5–75 (5 % to 75 %) is a shorter alternative. Longer duration means more load cycles and higher liquefaction risk.

Ia 由 5 % 累積到 95 % 經過的時間，由 Husid 圖讀取。以能量定義、不需門檻，也與振幅無關（紀錄乘幾倍，H(t) 不變），是最穩定的延時參數；另有 D5–75。延時越長、循環越多，越容易軟化、液化。HW1 中稱 Ds。

3.2 →延時 →
8Bracketed duration Db括號延時

The time between the first and the last exceedance of a threshold acceleration (Bolt 1973).The threshold is 0.05 g (absolute) or 5 % of PGA (relative) and must always be stated.Weak intervals in between are still counted, so a single late spike can lengthen it a lot.

第一次到最後一次超過門檻之間經過的時間（Bolt 1973）。門檻 0.05 g 或 5 % PGA，作答要寫明；中間變弱也照算，一個晚到的尖峰就能拉長很多。

3.2 →
9Uniform duration Du均勻延時

The sum of all time intervals during which |a(t)| exceeds the threshold (Sarma & Casey 1990).It counts only the time of actual strong shaking, so Du ≤ Db.With an absolute threshold both Du and Db increase when the record is scaled up.

|a| 超過門檻的所有時段加總，只算真正在強搖的時間，一定 ≤ Db。用絕對門檻時，紀錄放大會讓延時變長。

3.2 →
10Baseline drift (integration drift)HW1基線漂移（積分漂移）

A small constant offset ε in the recorded acceleration becomes a linear error εt in velocity and a quadratic error ½εt² in displacement after integration.In the frequency domain integration divides by iω, so the lowest frequencies are amplified most.Correction: remove the mean → band-pass filter (e.g. 0.1–30 Hz) → integrate → high-pass after each integration; real motion below the cut-off (including a fling step) is also removed.

加速度的微小零點偏差 ε，積分後在速度變成 εt、位移變成 ½εt²。積分 = 除以 iω，越低頻放大越多。處理：去平均 → 帶通 → 積分 → 每次積分後高通；代價是低於截止頻率的真實運動（含 fling step）也被濾掉。HW1 手機紀錄直接積分 161.7 s，位移誤差達 −142.6 m。

3.3 →
11Near-fault effect★近斷層效應

Special characteristics of ground motions recorded close to the fault rupture (roughly Rrup < 15–20 km), which far-field records do not have.Forward directivity: rupture propagating toward the site produces a short, large-amplitude velocity pulse, strongest in the fault-normal component.Fling step: permanent (step-like) ground displacement in the slip direction.Hanging-wall effect: larger motions on the hanging wall of a dipping fault; vertical motions are also large.The long-period pulse (1–5 s) is especially damaging to tall buildings, base-isolated structures and long-span bridges; distance must be measured to the rupture surface, not to the epicentre.

距破裂面很近（約 15–20 km 內）的特殊地動：前進方向性造成短延時、大振幅的速度脈衝（垂直斷層走向分量最大）；fling step 留下階梯狀永久位移；上盤地動較大；垂直分量大。脈衝週期 1–5 s，對高樓、隔震建築、長跨橋最不利。距離用到破裂面的 Rrup，不是震央距。

3.4 →
12Forward and backward directivity前進與後退方向性

Forward: rupture and slip propagate toward the site at nearly the S-wave velocity, so S waves from along the fault arrive almost together and add up into one large, short velocity pulse.Backward: rupture propagates away from the site; the same energy arrives over a longer time, giving small amplitude and long duration with no pulse.

前進：破裂朝工址、速度接近 S 波速，沿途發出的 S 波幾乎同時抵達、疊成一個大脈衝。後退：破裂背離工址，能量分散在較長時間，振幅小、延時長。

3.4 →
13Fling step滑移效應

The permanent ground displacement of a site that moves with one side of the fault.It appears as a step in the displacement history (a one-sided pulse in velocity), in the direction of slip: parallel to strike for strike-slip faults, in the dip direction for dip-slip faults.High-pass filtering removes it, so near-fault records need special baseline processing.

工址跟著斷層一側永久錯動，位移歷時出現階梯（速度上是單向脈衝），方向為滑移方向。高通濾波會把它濾掉。

3.4 →
14Hanging-wall effect上盤效應

For a dipping fault, sites on the hanging wall lie above the rupture plane and are closer to it than footwall sites at the same surface distance.So hanging-wall ground motions are larger (e.g. east of the Chelungpu fault in the 1999 Chi-Chi earthquake).

傾斜斷層的上盤在破裂面正上方，到破裂面的距離比同樣地表距離的下盤短，地動較大（1999 集集地震車籠埔斷層東側）。

3.4 →
15Fourier amplitude spectrum (FAS)HW1傅立葉振幅譜

A plot of the amplitudes of the harmonic components of a ground motion versus frequency, obtained by the Fourier transform: \(FAS(f_k) = \Delta t\,|X_k|\).It shows the frequency content of the motion itself (no SDOF amplification) and loses the time information.The highest analysable frequency is fN = 1/(2Δt); the resolution Δf = 1/(NΔt) depends only on the record length.

把地動拆成各頻率的簡諧成分，振幅對頻率作圖，描述地動本身的頻率內容；看不出何時發生。最高頻率 fN 由取樣率決定，解析度只由紀錄長度決定。

5.1 →
16Nyquist frequency and aliasingHW1Nyquist 頻率與混疊

fN = fs/2 = 1/(2Δt) is the highest frequency a sampled record can represent.A component above fN does not disappear; it is aliased to a false lower frequency, \(f_a = |f - f_s\,\mathrm{round}(f/f_s)|\).Strong-motion instruments use an analog anti-aliasing low-pass filter before sampling.

取樣紀錄能表示的最高頻率。高於 fN 的成分不會消失，而是變成假的低頻（例：fs = 100 Hz 量 60 Hz，看起來是 40 Hz）。強震儀取樣前先做類比低通濾波。

5.1 →
17Response spectrum反應譜

A plot of the peak response (displacement, pseudo-velocity or pseudo-acceleration) of a series of linear SDOF oscillators with the same damping ratio (usually 5 %) but different natural periods, all subjected to the same ground motion.It describes the effect of the motion on structures, including resonance amplification.At T → 0 the oscillator moves with the ground and Sa → PGA; at long periods Sa drops and Sd → PGD.

同一阻尼比（常用 5 %）、不同自然週期的線性 SDOF 受同一地動時的最大反應，對週期作圖；描述地動對結構的效果，含共振放大。週期極短端 Sa → PGA，長週期端 Sd → 地表最大位移。

5.2 →
18Spectral displacement, pseudo-velocity and pseudo-acceleration譜位移、擬速度、擬加速度

Sd is the peak relative displacement of the oscillator; \(PS_v = \omega_nS_d\) and \(PS_a = \omega_n^2S_d\), with ωn = 2π/Tn."Pseudo" because they are derived from Sd, not the true peak velocity or acceleration (nearly equal for light damping).kSd = mPSa is the maximum spring force, i.e. the equivalent static force used in seismic design.

Sd 是 SDOF 的最大相對位移；PSv = ωnSd、PSa = ωn²Sd。「擬」是因為由 Sd 換算，不是真正的最大速度、加速度（小阻尼時很接近）。kSd = mPSa 就是彈簧最大力，也就是規範的等效靜力。

5.2 →
19Predominant period Tp and mean period TmHW1卓越週期與平均週期

Tp: the period at which the Fourier amplitude spectrum is largest, or (other common definition) the period of the peak of the 5 %-damped acceleration response spectrum.Tm = ΣCi²(1/fi)/ΣCi² for 0.25 ≤ fi ≤ 20 Hz (Rathje et al. 1998): the mean period weighted by the squared Fourier amplitudes.Both are longer on soft soils and for larger earthquakes (e.g. Loma Prieta: rock Tp = 0.39 s, soil 1.5 s).

Tp：Fourier 振幅最大的週期，或 5 % 阻尼加速度反應譜峰值的週期（作答寫明用哪一種）。Tm：以 Fourier 振幅平方加權的平均週期（0.25–20 Hz）。軟土、大地震兩者都較長。

5.3 →3.2 →
20Fundamental period of a soil layer土層基本週期

A uniform soil layer of thickness H on rigid bedrock has \(T = 4H/V_s\).The layer amplifies ground-motion components near this period: the thicker and softer the layer, the longer the period.Buildings of about 0.1N s (N = number of storeys) near this period resonate with the site, as in Mexico City 1985 (6–15-storey buildings).

均勻土層在剛性岩盤上的週期 T = 4H/Vs；土層越厚越軟，週期越長，接近該週期的成分被放大。建物週期約 0.1 × 樓層數，接近時結構與土層一起共振（1985 墨西哥市倒的是 6–15 層樓）。

C　延時

#名稱公式與說明推導／練習
C1Husid function and significant duration★Husid 曲線與顯著延時
\[ \begin{aligned} &H(t) = \frac{\int_0^t a^2\,d\tau}{\int_0^{t_d}a^2\,d\tau} \\ &D_{5\text{–}95} = t_{95} - t_5,\qquad D_{5\text{–}75} = t_{75} - t_5 \end{aligned} \]

3.2 →練習 2.1練習 2.2
C2Bracketed duration of a sinusoid正弦紀錄的括號延時
\[ \begin{aligned} &D_b = t_{last} - t_{first} \\ &A\sin\omega t = a_t \;\Rightarrow\; t = \frac{1}{\omega}\sin^{-1}\frac{a_t}{A} \end{aligned} \]

tfirst: first crossing in the first segment with A > at; tlast: last crossing at the end of the last such segment (segment end − sin⁻¹(at/A)/ω).

第一次超過：第一個 A > at 段的開頭；最後一次：最後一個 A > at 段的結尾往前推。A < at 的段完全不算。

The bracket is the fraction of time a sinusoid of amplitude A spends above at.

括號內是正弦超過門檻的時間比例；單元 3.2 的例子：0.05 g 門檻 Du = 6.1 s、Db = 7.9 s。

簡答題的關鍵觀念

名詞解釋之外，常被問「為什麼」。每題先寫結論，再寫一句理由。

#問題答題重點詳細說明
1Do two records with the same PGA cause the same damage?PGA 相同，破壞一樣嗎？

No. PGA describes one instant; damage also depends on frequency content and duration.Three aspects are needed: amplitude (PGA, PGV, PGD), frequency content (FAS, Tp, Tm, response spectrum) and duration (D5–95, number of cycles).Cumulative measures (Ia, CAV) combine amplitude and duration and predict liquefaction better.

不一樣。振幅、頻率、延時三者缺一不可；Ia、CAV 綜合振幅與延時，對液化等累積損害較好。

3.1 →3.2 →
2Why are the rock and soil records different?岩盤與土壤紀錄為什麼不同？

The soil layer filters out part of the high frequencies and amplifies components near its own period 4H/Vs.So a soil site may have a smaller PGA but larger PGV, PGD, PGV/PGA and a longer Tp (Loma Prieta Gilroy: rock 0.39 s, soil 1.5 s).

土層濾掉部分高頻、放大接近自身週期的成分：PGA 可能較小，PGV、PGD、PGV/PGA 較大，卓越週期較長。

3.1 →5.3 →
3Which duration depends on amplitude?哪一種延時和振幅有關？

Db and Du with an absolute threshold (0.05 g) increase when the record is scaled up, because more peaks exceed the threshold.D5–95 (and Db, Du with a relative 5 % PGA threshold) do not change, because H(t) is a ratio.HW1: Ds vs PGA r ≈ −0.11 (nearly uncorrelated).

絕對門檻下 Db、Du 隨振幅變長；D5–95 是比例，振幅乘幾倍都不變。

3.2 →
4Why does direct integration of an accelerogram drift, and how is it fixed?直接積分為什麼漂移？怎麼處理？

A small offset ε (sensor bias, tilt) grows to εt in velocity and ½εt² in displacement; integration divides by iω and amplifies low frequencies.Fix: remove the mean, band-pass (0.1–30 Hz), integrate (trapezoidal), high-pass after each integration.Cost: real motion below the cut-off, such as a fling step, is also removed, so the displacement is only a relative vibration amplitude.

偏差在積分中變成直線、拋物線。去平均 → 帶通 → 積分 → 高通；代價是低頻真實運動（fling step）也被濾掉。

3.3 →
5Fourier spectrum vs response spectrumFourier 譜與反應譜的差別

Fourier spectrum: amplitude of each frequency component of the ground motion itself; plotted against frequency; no structure involved.Response spectrum: peak response of a series of SDOF oscillators (same ξ, different Tn); includes resonance amplification and represents the effect on structures; plotted against period.Neither keeps the timing of the motion.

Fourier 譜是地動本身的頻率成分；反應譜是一排 SDOF 的最大反應，含共振放大，代表對結構的效果。兩者都看不出何時發生。

5.1 →5.2 →2.2 (e) →
6Why is the near-fault pulse dangerous even if it is short?近斷層脈衝很短，為什麼還危險？

Its period (1–5 s) matches the natural periods of tall buildings, base-isolated buildings and long-span bridges, raising the long-period part of the response spectrum.It does not need cycles to build up resonance: one large velocity pulse delivers a large amount of energy at once.It is strongest in the fault-normal direction; seismic codes increase the design spectral coefficients near active faults.

脈衝週期正好打中長週期結構；不靠共振累積，一次給出大速度、大能量。垂直斷層走向最強；規範對近斷層區提高設計譜係數。

3.4 →
7Why use the rupture distance Rrup?為什麼用到破裂面的距離？

A large earthquake ruptures a fault tens of kilometres long; a site far from the epicentre can still be right next to the rupture.Near-fault effects and attenuation are controlled by the distance to the rupture surface, not to the epicentre.

大地震破裂面長達數十公里，離震央很遠的工址也可能就在破裂面旁邊。

3.4 →
8Why is CAV5 preferred to CAV, and Ia to PGA, for liquefaction?為什麼液化評估偏好 CAV5、Ia？

Liquefaction is cumulative: pore pressure builds up cycle by cycle, so both amplitude and duration matter; PGA captures only amplitude.CAV keeps growing during a long weak tail that does not affect the soil; CAV5 ignores |a| < 5 cm/s².

液化是逐圈累積的，要同時看振幅與延時；CAV 的弱尾段對土壤沒影響，CAV5 把它排除。

3.2 →
9Why did mid-rise buildings collapse in Mexico City (1985)?墨西哥市為什麼倒中高樓？

The soft lake clay amplified long-period motion near the site period (T = 4H/Vs), even 350 km from the epicentre.6–15-storey buildings (T ≈ 0.1N ≈ 0.6–1.5 s) were close to the site period and resonated with it; low buildings were far from it.

軟黏土湖積層放大接近其週期的長週期地動，中高樓週期接近而共振；低矮房子週期短，避開了。

常見陷阱與計算機提醒

  • 先換單位：題目給 g，積分前換成 m/s²（× 9.81）；Ia 的 π/2g 要和加速度用同一個 g。CAV 用 g 算出的是 g·s，要 × 9.81 才是 m/s。
  • 正弦積分只對整數週期成立：∫(A sin ωt)²dt = A²T/2、∫|A sin ωt|dt = 2AT/π。
  • Ia 與 CAV 的振幅次方不同：振幅 × k，Ia × k²、CAV × k；D5–95 不變。
  • Db、Du 要寫門檻：0.05 g 與 5 % PGA 可差兩倍以上；振幅低於門檻的段完全不算。
  • D5–95 用 Ia 累積，不是時間比例：內插依「段內 Ia 線性累積」，各段的 Ia 差很多。
  • Tp 有兩種定義：Fourier 譜峰值或 5 % 阻尼 Sa 峰值，作答寫明；Tm 只用 0.25–20 Hz。
  • ω 與 f 差 2π：PSv = ωnSd，ωn = 2π/Tn，不是 1/Tn。
  • RotD100 ≠ 兩分量取大：是旋轉到最不利方向的值，等於合成向量的最大值。
  • 近斷層距離看破裂面：用 Rrup，不是震央距；高通濾波會濾掉 fling step。
  • 垂直分量是 U-D：原講義 Ch2 p.5、10 標成 E-W 是錯的。
